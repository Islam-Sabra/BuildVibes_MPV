## How they go live
1. The founder takes both to a lawyer in Kuwait. The lawyer corrects them and answers the questions marked **[Lawyer]**.
2. The final English is translated into Arabic (the glossary's words; the Arabic is equal in force, or the lawyer says which one wins).
3. Both languages are published as web pages (for example on aecox.com).
4. Their links go into the Kuwait market pack, `markets.legal` (`{"terms": {"en", "ar"}, "privacy": {"en", "ar"}}`), in a reviewed migration. The app already shows them under Settings → About BuildVibes and legal, and on the sign-in screen. Nothing is typed in the app (rule 5).
5. Needed before the store submission (13–19 Dec): Apple and Google ask for the privacy link, and Apple asks how a person deletes their account (the app has it).

Egypt gets its own pages later, from its own pack.
