# BuildVibes — Privacy policy (DRAFT for legal review)

*Last updated: [date]. Applies in: Kuwait.*

**[Company]** ("we") runs BuildVibes. This policy says what we keep about you, why, who sees it, and what you can do. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

## 1. What we keep
| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your email address | To send your sign-in code. Nobody else sees it. |
| Your name, photo, country, role or trade, company, headline, experience and projects | Your profile. You choose what it shows. |
| A mobile number, if you add one | Private: only you can see it. |
| What you post: work items, checklists, notes, photos, files, voice notes, messages, comments | To show them to the people you shared them with. |
| When a proof photo was taken, and the place you type | The work item's record. The app doesn't ask for your location. |
| Your phone's push code, its type and the app's language | To send notifications. |
| Your private notebook | Only you can see it. |
| Reports and blocks | To keep people safe. |
| Basic logs (sign-ins, errors) | To keep the app working and secure. |
| Photos: the position the camera stored in them | **Not kept.** The app removes it on your phone before a photo is sent. |

We don't use your phone's contacts. We don't track you across other apps.

## 2. Who sees what
- **Your profile:** the people you allow, in the privacy settings.
- **A Space:** its members, by their pass; a Team's posts only that Team and the Space admin.
- **Direct messages:** only you and the other person. A message from someone you don't work with arrives as a request; its photos stay covered until you tap them.
- **Our team:** only when needed. We read reported content to decide on a report. A reported direct message shows only that message, never the whole conversation.
- **Service providers** who run parts of BuildVibes for us, under contract:
  - Supabase: the database, files and sign-in. Servers in India, Mumbai region.
  - Resend: sends the sign-in code email.
  - Google Firebase and Apple: deliver the notifications.
  - Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports from the app. They carry the type of error and where in the app it happened, never your name, an id or anything you typed.
- **The law:** when a Kuwaiti court or authority lawfully orders it.

## 3. How long we keep it
- While your account is open.
- **Deleting your account** (Settings → Account): your profile is hidden at once. You can restore it by signing in within **30 days**. After that, your account, profile, public posts, follows, notebook and photos are deleted for good.
- **Work in a Space stays** in that Space's record, shown as "Former member", because the team needs a whole work record. **[Lawyer]** Please confirm this is acceptable.
- Backups are kept for **[days]** and then overwritten.

## 4. Your choices and rights
- See and change your profile at any time.
- **Download your data:** Settings → Privacy. We send it to your email within 7 days.
- Turn notifications on or off, by kind and by Space.
- Block anyone, and report anything.
- Delete your account.
- Ask us anything at [Email].

## 5. Safety
- Every permission is checked on our servers, not only in the app.
- Data is sent encrypted. Files are reached only through short-lived links.
