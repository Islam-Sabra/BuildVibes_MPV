# Space and Project model — decision (9 Oct 2026, founder-approved)

## The model in one line
**Work happens in Spaces. A project is only a name that people and Spaces point to.**

## Space
- Created by a person or a company.
- It has two parts:
  - **Chat:** discussions in Everyone and in Teams.
  - **Workspace:** work items that follow a light process.
- It can be linked to a project. This is optional.
- Only members can see it. A community Space can be Open or Invite only.

## Joining and permissions
**The Space admin confirms every join and sets each member's permissions.**

**Joining:**
1. A person asks to join, with an invite link, an invite code or a request.
2. The admin confirms.
3. The person is in.

Nobody joins a work Space without the admin's confirmation.

**Permissions:** the admin picks a preset (a "pass") when confirming, and can fine-tune it later.

| Preset | Typical people | Chat | Create work items | Mark own items Done | Check (close) items | Add files to Library | Invite (admin still confirms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | Site engineer, supervisor, coordinator | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Crew** | Foreman, technician, subcontractor | ✓ | — | ✓ | — | ✓ | — |
| **Viewer** | Client representative, supplier, reviewing consultant | ✓ | — | — | — | — | — |

**Admin** sits above the presets. The admin:
- confirms joins and sets permissions;
- manages Teams;
- links the project;
- sets Space settings: Access, Who can invite, Notifications;
- can make other admins.

**Other rules:**
- **Who checks an item:** by default, the person who created it. The admin can also let a Lead check items created by others.
- **Community Spaces:** the admin confirms joins. An Open community can turn on "Accept requests automatically"; new members then join as Viewer.

## Project (a definition only)
**What it holds:** name, type, location and dates.

**What it's used for:**
- CV and portfolio entries on person and company profiles;
- tagging public posts;
- linking Spaces.

**No owner, no admin, no actions.** That means:
- no Follow and no Claim my role;
- no Posts, Team or Spaces tabs;
- no members or requests;
- no Discreet settings and no phases.

**The project sheet is read-only.** It shows:
- the definition;
- people and companies who listed the project on their CV and chose to show it;
- public posts tagged to it.

**Duplicates:** "Is it already here?" runs before anyone creates a new project. Corrections go through "Suggest an edit", reviewed by BuildVibes in a back-office queue.

## Proof
You count as having worked on a project if either:
- an admin of a Space linked to that project confirmed you as a member, or
- a teammate confirmed you.

Work items you closed as Checked can be shown on your CV as evidence, only if you choose. The public view shows the title, photo, place and who checked it. The discussion, the files and the Space stay private.

## Work item process (V1)
**Fields:**
- title;
- photos or files;
- **one owner** (a single person);
- due date;
- Team (optional);
- place on site, such as level or zone (optional).

| Step | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** | A member allowed to create items | Shows in the owner's Needs you |
| **Done** | The owner | Adds a photo or note as evidence; the creator is notified |
| **Checked** | The creator, or a Lead the admin allowed | Accepts and closes it, or sends it back to Open with a reason |

- The discussion lives on the item. The item also shows as a card in the Space chat.
- **Not in V1:**
  - item types such as RFI, inspection request or NCR (these come later, as templates);
  - approvals by several people;
  - custom workflows;
  - Gantt charts and schedules.

## Free vs paid
- **V1 is free for everyone.**
- **Measure from day one:**
  - active Spaces per company;
  - members per Space;
  - work items closed per week;
  - storage used.
- The free vs paid decision is made after the pilot.
