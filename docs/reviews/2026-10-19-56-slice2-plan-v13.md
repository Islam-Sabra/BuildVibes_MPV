## Rules carried over from migration 19 and review 36
- **Ack faces** (Got it, On my way, In my calendar) are seen only by the post's author, or by an admin acting for an author who has left. Everyone else sees only their own button. K1 therefore shows no faces to Islam, who isn't the author.
- **Received faces on a file post** are seen by everyone who can see it (review 36 R1).
- **Never a count** except "2 of 5 ticked" on a checklist. That counts the checklist's own points, so it is progress, not a quality signal.
- A checklist with unticked points is in its people's Needs you, with the paper clip on the card.
