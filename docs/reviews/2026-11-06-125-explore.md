# Review 125: Explore, the public side of BuildVibes (decisions)

**From:** the lead, after a grilling round with the founder (6 Nov, 14:38–14:50). **Status:** DECISIONS SETTLED. Frames and the build plan follow in this file, then Cursor.
This **replaces review 117** (people-only Explore, on hold). Review 117's people search becomes one part of this.

## What Explore is (the founder's words, made exact)
- Explore **acts like a feed**, but **nothing on it is permanent**. Every post lasts **24 hours**, like a status.
- **Exceptions:**
  - tenders and events last **until their closing or event date**;
  - jobs, after the pilot, last until filled or 30 days at most.
- Explore **replaces the Home feed** as the public side of the app. Home stays the Spaces, and the old feed stays hidden.
- **Country first:**
  - a post tagged with a country shows **only to people in that country** (from the profile's market);
  - a post tagged **General** (the industry worldwide) shows to everyone.

## Also in the pilot (lead's proposal, founder "yes", 14:50)
- **Site alerts** «تنبيهات الموقع»: BuildVibes posts for a country, such as the midday outdoor work ban, a dust storm or heat warning, or changed working hours. They're **pinned on top while they apply**. The rules and dates come from the market pack (rule 5).
- **Training and events** «تدريب وفعاليات»: from companies or BuildVibes. They last **until the event date**.
- **This week in construction** «هذا الأسبوع في الإنشاءات»: one BuildVibes post a week per country with the news, decisions and prices that matter.

## Rules that bind the design
- **Rule 2:**
  - posting a 24-hour post takes three taps: camera or text, then post;
  - reading needs no English.
- **Rule 3:**
  - no view counts, no reaction counts, no follower numbers on posts;
  - no rank order in "Active this week".
- **Rule 4:** Near you and Open to work are by choice, and a company's facts show only if its admin publishes them.
- **Rule 5:** country, alerts, working-hour rules and price sources come from the market pack or the post's country tag.
- **Rule 9:**
  - images and videos are compressed on the phone before upload;
  - videos are 30 s at most, 720p, with no autoplay on mobile data;
  - expired media is **deleted from storage** by a cron job, not just hidden.

## One product language (founder, 6 Nov)
- **Every place you write in BuildVibes uses the same composer:** the top bar with ✕ and Discard, writing in place, chips for what's attached, pinned lines (Type, For, Post as…), and the bar above the keyboard with Send.
- **A surface may be simpler**, with fewer lines and fewer bar buttons, **but never a different style.** The first EX3 (big tiles and a form) broke this and was replaced.
- **Rule for every new frame:** start from the closest existing frame or component, and remove what isn't needed. Never invent a new look.

## Who sees a post: the app decides, never the poster (founder, 6 Nov)
- **People and companies don't choose an audience.** The "Shows to" choice was removed from EX3.
- **BuildVibes posts (EX7) keep their country or General tag.** That describes the content (a Kuwait decision belongs to Kuwait); it isn't the poster picking an audience.
- **How Explore orders and spreads posts, for the pilot.** The rules run on the server, in one function `explore_feed(p_before)`, and are easy to change later.
  1. **Site alerts** for the viewer's country, while they apply, on top.
  2. **BuildVibes posts:** the viewer's country, then General.
  3. **Posts from people and companies the viewer is connected to:** shares a Space with, or follows.
  4. **Posts by authors in the viewer's country** (the author's market from the profile), newest first.
  5. **Posts from other countries** only when they reach beyond their own country: from a registered company, or reacted to by at least 5 different people within the 24 hours. **This number stays inside the server and is never shown** (rule 3).
  - **Hidden always:** blocked people, posts hidden by reports, and the viewer's own reported posts.
- **No personal settings for this** (rule 7).
