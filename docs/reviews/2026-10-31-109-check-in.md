# Review 109: Check-in (31 Oct 2026)

**Founder, 09:02–09:13:** a Check-in post in the Space, kept simple, not a SaaS screen. It has a check-in time with an allowance, an optional check-out with an allowance, and Once or Every day. A strong notification lets people check in from outside the app. A warning comes before the location is read, and refusing is allowed. Only the creator sees who checked in, and the day's list is saved to the Library for him only.

**Status: approved (founder, 09:13: "ok"). Build before the pilot, after review 108.**

## 1. The composer (CI1, CI1b), redrawn 1 Nov in review 110's composer
- **Check-in** («تسجيل حضور») is a tile in the **#** grid, shown **only to Leads**. A non-Lead's last kind never opens on it (review 110 W11).
- **Top bar:** "New check-in" and the Space.
- **Writing:** the title, then Details (optional).
- **Shown by default:**
  - **For:** teams or the whole Space, the same picker as Team. Viewers are never asked. Review 112's "not for yourself" is about work items and doesn't apply here.
  - **Check-in:** a time and an allowance (0 · 10 · 15 · 30 min, one choice), for example 7:00 · 15 min.
  - **Place:** **Set place here** reads the Lead's location once, when tapped. That point is the post's site. Send stays off until it's set.
- **+ for a check-in (CI1b):**
  - **Check-out** adds a time and an allowance.
  - **Every day** sets the market pack's working days, never typed in code. Without it, the check-in happens once.
  - Each shows as a chip with **×** once added.
  - For two shifts, the Lead posts two Check-ins.
- **The bar:** # · + · Send. There's no camera for a check-in. Hold Send = Post later, as for every kind.
- **Fixed by the app, with no settings:** the distance allowed is **100 m**.

## 2. The times
- **Check-in opens** at the time minus the allowance (6:45). It is **on time** up to the time plus the allowance (7:15) and **late** after that. It stays open until check-out opens, or until the end of the day when there's no check-out.
- **Check-out opens** at its time minus the allowance (14:45) and stays open to the end of the day. Checking out within the allowance is on time.
- **Missed:** nobody checked in before check-in closed. A missing Out is shown as no Out stamp.
- All times are in the Space's time zone (`space_time_zone`).

## 3. The person (CI2, CI3, CI4)
- **The notification:**
  - When check-in opens, a **strong notification** arrives even if the app is closed. It uses its own channel with sound and high priority, and on iOS it's time-sensitive. It shows the title, the Space, "by {on-time limit}", and two actions: **Check in · Open**.
  - The same happens when check-out opens, with **Check out**.
- **Check in from the notification:**
  - **On Android,** it finishes without opening the app.
  - **On iPhone,** the app opens for a moment to read the location; Apple requires that.
  - Either way, the result arrives as a notification: "Checked in 6:58 · on site".
- **The first time only (CI3),** a warning: "Checking in shares your location. The app reads where you are once, now, and tells the creator if you are at the site. It does not follow you after that."
  - **Accept:** the phone's permission prompt follows, once. Then the location is read.
  - **Check in without location:** they're checked in, marked "location not shared". This choice is remembered: the next time they get the warning again, until they accept.
  - If they later turn location off in the phone settings, they're checked in without location, with no error screen.
- **Their card (CI4)** shows their own stamp only:
  - ✓ On time · on site (green);
  - Late (amber);
  - Not at site (amber), with **Try again** while check-in is open;
  - Location not shared (grey).
  - Then **Check out** (disabled until its time).
- A weak GPS signal is retried once quietly, then counts as "location not shared".

## 4. The creator (CI5)
- **Only the person who posted it** sees the list, plus the **Space admin** if the creator has left the Space. Nobody else sees it, not other Leads and not the crew.
- **One line per person:** name, the In stamp and the Out stamp. A day picker at the top goes back through earlier days.
- **No notification for each person.** He gets one when check-in closes: "Morning, Block B: 2 late · 1 not at site · 1 missed". It's only sent when there's something to see.
- **Every evening** (at the end of the day in the Space's time zone), the day's list is saved to the **Library** as a spreadsheet (.xlsx): name, team, In, Out, late, place result. It goes into a "Check-in reports" folder that **only the creator can open**. That's a new per-file "only me" visibility, used only here.

## 5. Fixed rules (no settings)
- **The location:**
  - It is read once per tap, never tracked, and there's **no background location** permission.
  - **The person's exact point is never stored.** Only the result (on site / not at site / not shared) and the time are kept.
- **Not proof:** check-ins never show on the profile.
- **Stopping it:** the creator can **Stop** a repeating Check-in from its ⋯ menu; earlier days stay in the Library.

## 6. Server (one migration + one function)
- **Post kind `check_in`.**
- **Table `check_in_posts`:** `post_id`, `site_lat`, `site_lng` (the site, not a person), `team_ids null`, `in_time`, `in_allow_min`, `out_time null`, `out_allow_min null`, `repeat` ('once' | 'daily'), `stopped_at null`.
- **Table `check_in_marks`:** `post_id`, `user_id`, `day`, `kind` ('in' | 'out'), `at`, `on_time bool`, `place` ('on_site' | 'away' | 'not_shared'), with a unique (`post_id`, `user_id`, `day`, `kind`). **There are no person coordinates anywhere.**
- **Functions** (security definer, `search_path = ''`):
  - **`post_check_in(...)`:** Leads only.
  - **`mark_check_in(post, kind, lat null, lng null)`:** the caller must be in an asked team. It checks the time against the rules in part 2 and computes the 100 m test in SQL (haversine). **It never stores the lat/lng it was given.** An 'away' result can be replaced while check-in is open.
  - **`stop_check_in(post)`:** the creator only.
- **RLS:** a mark row is readable by its own person, the post's creator, and the Space admin when the creator has left the Space. Nothing else can read it, and inserts and updates go only through the functions.
- **Cron:**
  - `check_in_notify()` every minute sends the opening notifications and the creator's summary.
  - `check_in_daily_report()` at the end of the day calls an edge function that writes the .xlsx into the Library with "only me" visibility.
- **pgTAP:**
  - a non-member, a Viewer and a Crew member not in the asked teams can't mark;
  - Crew can't read another person's marks;
  - another Lead can't read the list;
  - the creator can;
  - on time and late at the exact edges;
  - 'missed';
  - no coordinates are stored;
  - the "only me" Library file is invisible to other members.

## 7. App

- **Notifications:** a new high-priority "Check-in" channel on Android, and time-sensitive on iOS (this needs the Time Sensitive capability).

## The lead's decisions during the build (1 Nov)
1. **Pushes follow the lock-screen rule (migration 14).**
   - With lock-screen text on, a push shows the title and the Space.
   - With it off, it says only "Time to check in" / "Time to check out" («حان وقت تسجيل الحضور» / «حان وقت تسجيل الخروج»), or for the creator "Check-in summary ready" («ملخص الحضور جاهز»), with the same actions.
   - The app draws exactly the server's words. Its own "Checked in…" notification follows the same rule.
2. **One Team asked = a Team post** (seen by that Team, the creator and the admins).
3. **Stop:** the creator, or a Space admin once the creator has left. The card carries `can_stop`.
4. **Data:**
   - **Export my data** includes the person's own marks.
   - **A deleted account** takes its "only me" reports with it (the Library rows and the .xlsx files).
