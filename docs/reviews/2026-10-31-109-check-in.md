## Decisions (founder, 31 Oct)
- **A. One reading at the tap.** The location is never tracked before or after. No background location permission.
- **B. What is kept:** the distance in metres, the result, the time and the "may be faked" flag. **The person's exact point is never stored**, not even in logs.
- **C. One post is one check-in.** For a daily one, the Lead schedules it with Post later (review 108) or uses a template. Repeating work items stay out of scope.
- **D. Not proof.** A check-in never shows on the profile as proof. Proof stays for checked work items.

## 1. Writing a Check-in (CI1)
- **Check-in** («تسجيل وصول») is a new kind chip in the Space's writing sheet, after Checklist. **Only Leads** see it.
- The title is the first line, as for every post. The hint reads "Each person taps Check in when they arrive. Their phone reads the location once."
- **Place** is one single choice (rule 6): **Project site · Set it here**.
  - **Set it here:** the Lead's phone reads its location once, when the post is sent. That point becomes the post's target, and also the Space's saved **site point** (it replaces any earlier one).
  - **Project site:** uses the Space's saved site point. It's offered only once one exists; until then, only "Set it here" shows.
  - The site point is the place's point, not a person's, so it's fine to store it.
- **Within** is one single choice: **50 m · 100 m · 250 m**, with 100 m as the default. The three values live in the specs, not typed into widgets. "m" and "km" come from l10n («م», «كم»).
- **Team:** one or more teams, or the whole Space. It uses the same picker as work items. Viewers are never asked to check in.
- **Time:** optional; a start and an end on one day, in the Space's time zone (`space_time_zone`). If it's empty, the check-in is open until the Lead closes it.
- **Post**, or Post later from review 108.

## 2. The member's card (CI2)
- The card shows the kind line (pin icon, "Check-in"), the title, and the facts: place and distance, time, teams.
- For each person asked to check in who hasn't yet, it counts in **Needs you**.
- **The button, Check in («سجّل وصولي»),** is full width with the brand fill. It's disabled before the window opens ("Opens at 6:30") and gone after the post closes.
- The line under the button reads "Your location is read once, when you tap. It is not followed afterwards."
- **The first tap asks for location permission** ("while using the app" only). If it's refused, the card shows "Turn on location for BuildVibes to check in" with a button to the phone's settings.
- A push goes to the people asked when the window opens, or when the post is sent if there's no window: "Check in: {title}".

## 3. The result stamps (CI3)
- **The server decides**, never the app. The app sends one reading (latitude, longitude, accuracy in metres, and Android's mock flag) to `check_in(post_id, …)`.
- **On site · {distance} · {time}** (green): the distance is within the radius. This is final; the button goes.
- **Away from site · {distance} · {time}** (amber): it is saved, and **Try again** replaces it with a new reading, which may turn it into On site.
- **Location not clear** (grey): the reading's accuracy is worse than the radius. **Nothing is saved.** The card shows "Step outside and try again."
- **Location may be faked** (amber, next to the result): shown when Android reports a mock location. It's stored as a flag and shown to the person and to Leads. iPhones give no such signal, so it never shows there.
- Distances show as whole metres under 1,000 m, then km with one decimal, always in Western digits.

## 4. The opened Check-in (CI4)
- At the top are the facts. Then three groups: **Checked in · Away · Not yet**, each a list of people with their team and stamp.
  - **Leads** see everyone.
  - **Crew** see only their own stamp. This is the disclosure-by-choice rule: nobody sees a colleague's distance.
- **Remind** on a "Not yet" row sends that person a reminder at once, through the same push path as review 108's Someone reminder. At most one reminder per person per 30 minutes.
- **Close check-in** (Leads only) ends it. "Not yet" people stay as "Didn't check in". There are no counts in the header, only the lists.
- The post can't be archived while it's open; once closed, it follows the normal Archive rule.

## 5. Server (one migration)
- **Posts:** a new post kind, `check_in`.
- **Table `check_in_targets`** (one row per post): `post_id`, `lat`, `lng` (the site point), `radius_m` (50 | 100 | 250), `opens_at null`, `closes_at null`, `closed_at null`, `closed_by null`, `team_ids` (null means the whole Space).
- **`spaces.site_lat`, `spaces.site_lng`:** the saved site point. These are set only through the post function.
- **Table `check_ins`:** `post_id`, `user_id`, `result` ('on_site' | 'away'), `distance_m int`, `accuracy_m int`, `maybe_mock bool`, `checked_at`, with a unique (`post_id`, `user_id`). **There are no person lat/lng columns.**
- **Functions** (security definer, `search_path = ''`):
  - **`post_check_in(space, title, place_mode, lat, lng, radius, teams, opens, closes)`:** Lead only. With `place_mode = 'here'` it saves the point on the Space too.
  - **`check_in(post, lat, lng, accuracy, is_mock)`:** the caller must be an active, non-Viewer member of an asked team, inside the window and with the post open. It computes the haversine distance in SQL (no PostGIS). It returns 'unclear' and stores nothing when the accuracy is worse than the radius. It **never writes the lat/lng it was given.** It replaces an earlier 'away' row and refuses after 'on_site'. Then it updates Needs you.
  - **`close_check_in(post)`:** Lead only.
  - **`remind_check_in(post, user)`:** Lead only, rate-limited.
- **RLS:**
  - `check_ins` rows are readable by their own person and by Leads of that Space, with no other read path.
  - `check_in_targets` is readable by Space members; the site point never reaches non-members.
  - Inserts and updates happen only through the functions.
- **pgTAP:**
  - non-members can't read or write anything;
  - a Viewer and a Crew member outside the asked teams can't check in;
  - Crew can't read another person's row;
  - unclear stores nothing;
  - on_site is final;
  - outside the window is refused;
  - a closed post is refused;
  - no column anywhere holds a person's coordinates.

## 6. App
- **Plugin:** `geolocator` (latest stable), foreground only.
  - **Android:** `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` and `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`. **No background permission.**
  - **iOS:** `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` in EN and AR: "BuildVibes reads your location once when you tap Check in, to confirm you are at the site."
- **The reading:** one high-accuracy reading with a 20-second timeout. A timeout counts as "Location not clear".
- **Tests:**
  - every state of CI2 and CI3 in EN and AR, with a fake location service;
  - the permission-refused card;
  - the window states;
  - Crew see only their own row in CI4.
