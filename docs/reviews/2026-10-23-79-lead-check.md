**Fix before the founder's test:**
- **A1. «المعتمدة» on the file page (LV2).** `libraryInUse` reads "Rev D · المعتمدة", which means "approved". The glossary forbids مُعتمد (a formal approval; approval codes are out of the MVP), and the English says "in use". Use **«{rev} · الحالية»**.
- **A2. Text chevrons point the wrong way in Arabic.** `daybookForChoose` «لـ: اختر ›», `daybookForPersonChoose`, `daybookForYouChoose` and `daybookComingChoose` draw › pointing right (DM5). In Arabic the arrow points left. Use the mirrored caret icon (as the To do docket does), not a character in the string. Check every ARB value for › or ‹. *(My own F66 wording wrote "لـ Islam Ahmed ›"; this corrects it.)*
- **A3. A 12-hour time wraps onto two lines in a card's header.** "8:14 / صباحًا" (N1, the card under it) and "2:10 / ظهرًا" (R1b). The time and «صباحًا/مساءً» stay on one line, beside each other (F53). Give the time its own width and never let it wrap.
- **A4. A Latin file name cut in an Arabic list puts "…" at the wrong end.** LV1 shows "…Method statement, sleeves". Give a Latin name its own left-to-right direction (the `bidi.dart` helper), so it is cut at its own end: "Method statement, sl…".
- **A5. «اقبله كـفريق تنفيذ» (P2)** has a tatweel. Use «اقبله كفريق تنفيذ».
- **A6. Reply has two icons:** a curved arrow in direct messages (DM3) and a speech bubble in the Space's chat (S8m). Use one icon everywhere. If it's the arrow, it mirrors in Arabic.

**Noted, no change:**
- The pass word **فريق تنفيذ** (Crew) sits next to **الفِرق** (Teams). Both are in the founder's glossary and the screens keep them in separate places (the pass tag and the Teams chips). The sample Team names in the screenshots ("فريق الإنشاءات") make it look busier than real names will.
