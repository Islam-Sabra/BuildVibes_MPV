### Does it build on Flutter 3.44?
**Yes, with one change.**
- **`agora_rtc_engine` 6.6.4** (latest) fails as shipped. Its Android module compiles against SDK 31 unless the root project names one, and today's AndroidX needs 34 or later. One line in `android/build.gradle.kts` (`extra["compileSdkVersion"] = 36`) fixes it.
- **`flutter_callkit_incoming` 3.1.6** (latest) builds. The only warning is the plugin's old Kotlin set-up.
- **`permission_handler`:** 13.0.2 needs compile SDK 37, so I pinned 12.0.1. The real feature may not need it at all.
- **Firebase:** the `.spike` package gets push tokens using the same Firebase app, with a second client entry in the local, git-ignored `google-services.json`. Nothing changed in the Firebase console.

**Notes on the numbers:**
- **Round trip (`gatewayRtt`)** read 0 throughout on both ends, so last-mile delay and end-to-end delay are used instead.
- **Frame rate:** the phone sent about 10 fps against the 30 asked for. Possible causes are the camera in indoor light or the encoder adapting. Not checked.
- **The echo:** the first try had echo and a whistle with the PC speakers and the phone in one room. After the cold boot with headphones:
  - phone → PC was clean;
  - PC → phone still echoed, with the Xiaomi's own microphone muted. So the loop is on the PC side, through the emulator's sound.
  An emulator using the PC's sound devices is a poor test of echo cancellation. **Echo needs a real phone-to-phone call, in two rooms.**
- **The emulator adds delay of its own.** End-to-end audio delay measured on the Pixel_8 side was ~670 ms in the voice call.

## App size (release, arm64)

| Build | APK on the phone | Download (compressed, estimate) |
|---|---|---|
| BuildVibes today (`slice2-app` b3b3409c) | 29.9 MB | ~14 MB |
| + Agora full SDK + callkit | **108.6 MB (+79 MB)** | **~58 MB (+43 MB)** |
| + Agora trimmed to what a call needs (estimate, not built) | ~68 MB | ~38 MB (+24 MB) |

- **Agora's full SDK** brings 72.5 MB of arm64 native libraries. The core library is 28.1 MB; the rest is 15 optional extensions:
  - lip sync 6.9 MB, clear vision 5.7 MB, spatial audio 4.6 MB;
  - face capture, segmentation, AV1 and others.
- **The "trimmed" row** keeps:
  - the core, `ffmpeg`, `fdkaac` and `soundtouch`;
  - AI noise suppression and AI echo cancellation;
  - the video encoder.

  It is computed from the built APK, not built.
- **About `--target-platform android-arm64`:** it doesn't drop Agora's prebuilt libraries for other CPUs. The fat release APK was 213 MB. The numbers above count arm64 only, which is what Play delivers per phone.

## Verdicts so far
1. **Do Agora calls work well on Kuwait mobile networks? Likely yes; not finished.**

   - Still to do: a real phone-to-phone call, a SIM in the phone, and the company Wi-Fi.

   - **Battery:** pending.
