### Does it build on Flutter 3.44?
**Yes, with one change.**
- **`agora_rtc_engine` 6.6.4** (latest) fails as shipped. Its Android module compiles against SDK 31 unless the root project names one, and today's AndroidX needs 34 or later. One line in `android/build.gradle.kts` (`extra["compileSdkVersion"] = 36`) fixes it.
- **`flutter_callkit_incoming` 3.1.6** (latest) builds. The only warning is the plugin's old Kotlin set-up.
- **`permission_handler`:** 13.0.2 needs compile SDK 37, so I pinned 12.0.1. The real feature may not need it at all.
- **Firebase:** the `.spike` package gets push tokens using the same Firebase app, with a second client entry in the local, git-ignored `google-services.json`. Nothing changed in the Firebase console.

**Notes on the numbers:**
- **Round trip (`gatewayRtt`)** read 0 throughout on both ends, so last-mile delay and end-to-end delay are used instead.
- **Frame rate:** the phone sent about 10 fps against the 30 asked for. Possible causes are the camera in indoor light or the encoder adapting. Not checked.
- **The echo:** the first try had echo and a whistle with the PC speakers and the phone in one room. After the cold boot with headphones:
  - phone → PC was clean;
  - PC → phone still echoed, with the Xiaomi's own microphone muted. So the loop is on the PC side, through the emulator's sound.
  An emulator using the PC's sound devices is a poor test of echo cancellation. **Echo needs a real phone-to-phone call, in two rooms.**
- **The emulator adds delay of its own.** End-to-end audio delay measured on the Pixel_8 side was ~670 ms in the voice call.
- **Rows 1–3 ran on the full SDK.** The trimmed build was checked afterwards with a video call between two emulators: 720p both ways, 0% loss, no crash. It wasn't checked in a call on the Xiaomi.

## App size (release, arm64)
**Measured:** the real arm64 APK that `--split-per-abi` builds, for BuildVibes itself (`lib/main.dart`) with and without Agora.

**Download** is that APK compressed (deflate), an estimate of what Play sends. I couldn't fetch bundletool for Play's own number: the download was blocked on the PC.

| Build | APK on the phone (≈ installed) | Download (estimate) |
|---|---|---|
| BuildVibes today (`slice2-app` b3b3409c) | 30.0 MB | ~14.3 MB |
| + Agora full SDK + callkit | 108.6 MB (+79 MB) | ~57.5 MB (+43 MB) |
| **+ Agora trimmed + callkit (built)** | **71.2 MB (+41 MB)** | **~33.5 MB (+19 MB)** |

- **The trimmed build** leaves out every optional extension (`packaging.jniLibs.excludes += "**/libagora_*_extension.so"`), 14 files:
  - AI noise suppression, AI echo cancellation, audio beauty, clear vision, content inspection;
  - face capture, face detection, lip sync, segmentation, spatial audio, screen capture;
  - the video quality analyser, the AV1 encoder and the extra video encoder.
- **What stays:** 39.0 MB of Agora libraries.
  - the core, 28.1 MB;
  - `ffmpeg` 5.9 MB, `fdkaac` 0.7 MB and `soundtouch` 0.2 MB;
  - the Flutter bridge (`AgoraRtcWrapper`, `iris`) 2.3 MB;
  - `video_dec` 1.2 MB and `aosl` 0.6 MB.
- **`ffmpeg`, `fdkaac` and `soundtouch` can't go.** The core library links them (ELF `NEEDED`), and a first build without them crashed at start: `UnsatisfiedLinkError: libagora-ffmpeg.so not found`.
- **Installed size:**
  - Android keeps native libraries inside the APK without unpacking them, so the installed app is about the APK size: BuildVibes with trimmed Agora about 71 MB, against 30 MB today.
  - Checked on the Xiaomi: the trimmed test app takes 59.7 MB in `/data/app`.
- **Against the founder's target** (a download of no more than about 40 MB): trimmed Agora comes to about 33.5 MB. **It fits.** The full SDK (~57.5 MB) doesn't.
- **About `--target-platform android-arm64`:** it doesn't drop Agora's prebuilt libraries for other CPUs; the fat APK was 213 MB. Use `--split-per-abi` or an app bundle.
