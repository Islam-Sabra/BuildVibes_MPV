## Migration 26: approved to go live
On my side (Postgres 16 with the Supabase stand-in), every migration applies from scratch and **all 1,782 SQL checks pass** (26 files).

**What I checked:**
- The five functions that are created again (`sign_done`, `check_item`, `send_back`, `add_comment`, `reassign_work_item`) match their versions before migration 26 exactly, except for the one new line that locks the post. Nothing else changed in them.
- **Every writer is covered:**
  - **Events:** only those five and `create_work_item` write events, and the `raised` event belongs to a post nobody else can be cancelling yet.
  - **Edits:** only `edit_space_post` and `save_blocks` write edits. The edit triggers update the post in the same transaction.
- **`last_activity_at`:**
  - the app can read it but never write it (no update grant);
  - the trigger only raises it, never lowers it;
  - the back-fill takes the latest of the post, its events, its edits and its cancel.
- **`daybook_cards`:**
  - it is for members only and uses the same visibility as before;
  - one row comparison keeps the cursor in the index;
  - the history is limited to 50 lines, with `history_more`.
- **`post_cards` (F30):**
  - the latest reply follows `post_replies`' read policy, which hides deleted, blocked and reported replies;
  - the faces show at most three people, never a count;
  - `to_me` excludes your own replies.

## Fix before the phone build (branch `slice1-v13`)

### A1. One time format everywhere
The same app shows "14:00" in the daybook, "1:44 pm" and "6:10 am" on Home, and "06:10" on the post page.
- **Rule:** one formatter for every time in the app. It follows the phone's 24-hour setting (`MediaQuery.alwaysUse24HourFormat`).
  - In 12-hour mode, English shows "2:00 pm" and Arabic puts the period on a small second line (F15).
  - In 24-hour mode, both show "14:00".
- **Test:** the same time on a card, a history line, a feed card, a reply and an Activity row, in both modes and both languages.

### A2. The post page
- **No reply line on the post itself:** the replies are listed right under it, so the line only repeats them.
- **Each reply** uses the two sizes:
  - the name at the main size (medium);
  - the time small;
  - **Useful** and **Reply** at the main size, with 48-point tap areas (they are tiny today).
- The reply field's placeholder reads **"Write a reply"** / **"اكتب ردًا"**, not "Add a comment". The section is called Replies.
- Useful uses the seal-check icon on the feed card and the post page.

### A3. Arabic wording (gender-neutral, natural)
- **History lines on the card:** use the paper's ledger form, the name first, then the action as a noun. The passive "من قِبل" reads like a translation.
  - "عمر حداد · توقيع كمنجز: رُكّبت الست كلها."
  - "إسلام أحمد · إرجاع: الصورة غير موجودة"; your own lines say "أنت · إرجاع: …"
  - "سارة المطيري · تعديل الموعد: من الخميس إلى اليوم"
  - "عمر حداد · تحديث: وصلت الجلب. أركّبها الآن."
  - "إسلام أحمد · تحقّق"
- **"Useful":**
  - "منشورك مفيد لـراكيش ناير" and "مفيد لمريم الصباح وراكيش ناير" say that the post is useful *for* them.
  - Use **"علامة مفيد من مريم الصباح وراكيش ناير"** on the card, and **"علامة مفيد على منشورك من راكيش ناير"** in Activity.
- The other Activity lines are fine as they are (ردّ من…, بند عمل لك من…, متابِع جديد: …).

### A4. Words that don't match the glossary
- **People tab:** "Owners / Owner" → **"Space admin"** / **"مدير المساحة"**, as in Space info and the glossary.
- **Teams in People:** "2 members", "1 member" → up to three small faces, no number. It's the same pattern as replies and Got it (rule 3).
- **The Space header:** "Private work · project Space" → **"Private work"** / **"عمل خاص"**, as in the v13 frames.

### A5. Settings shows the same rows twice
- **Today:** ST1 lists Privacy, Notifications and Language under "Your settings, in words". It then lists Privacy and safety, Notifications and Language and region again under Account.
- **Fix:** one list, each entry once:
  - Account and security;
  - Notifications;
  - Privacy and safety;
  - Language;
  - About BuildVibes and legal.
- **"Components"** shows only in debug builds. Check that it is absent from the release build.

### A6. Home → Spaces date line
"THURSDAY 15 OCTOBER · 2 things need you" is in capitals. Use the same one-line date as Feed and the daybook: the day in bold, the date in grey, then "· 2 things need you" in the action colour.
