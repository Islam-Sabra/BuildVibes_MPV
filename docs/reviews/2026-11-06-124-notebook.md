**From:** the lead, from the founder's phone test of build 1097. **Status:** in progress. N2, the font, waits for the founder's screenshot.

## N1. Holding an entry shows two "Space" share icons while the feed is hidden
- **Seen by the founder:** hold an entry, and the row of actions shows two share icons that both lead to a Space.
- **Cause, in the code:**
  - `holdKinds()` in `lib/features/notebook/note_status.dart` returns **every** `NoteStatusKind`, `feed` included, whatever `feedEnabledProvider` says.
  - With the feed hidden, the feed action falls through to the Space share (`note_share.dart` around line 172), so two icons do the same thing.
  - The Share menu already hides Feed (review 113 (6)); the hold row was missed.
- **Fix:**
  - `holdKinds(note, {required bool feedOn})` leaves out `NoteStatusKind.feed` when the feed is off. The caller passes `ref.watch(feedEnabledProvider)`.
  - **Status icons of past shares are unchanged:** an entry already shared to the feed before it was hidden still shows its feed status, read-only, with no "Share again" while the feed is off.
- **Tests:**
  - widget: feed off → the hold row has Pin · Reminder · Space · Message, **no feed icon**;
  - feed on → five icons (four for a voice note);
  - a past feed share with the feed off → the status shows, no Share again.
  - EN and AR goldens.
