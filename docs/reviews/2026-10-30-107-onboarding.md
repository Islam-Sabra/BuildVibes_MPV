# Review 107: onboarding (polish queue item 1) — 30 Oct 2026

**Status: frames approved by the founder (30 Oct, 09:59).** The reference he sent is Facebook's entry: one welcome screen with one main button, language at the top, one question per screen, clear waiting states, and "Try another way".

## Today's flow (from the code on `slice2-app` and the frames E1–E10)
1. **E1 Welcome:** the title, a body paragraph, a rotating card of 6 audiences, then **Create account**, **Sign in** and **Skip to Home**.
2. **E2 Join or sign in:** the title, a body, a sample profile card, **Continue with email**, then Google and Apple (once their keys exist), the terms, and "Just looking".
3. **Email**, then **E3e the 6-digit code**, which moves on by itself.
4. **E9 About you (step 1 of 2):** first name, last name, country, role (opens E9b, the role picker), company (optional), mobile or email (optional). Six fields on one screen.
5. **E10 People to follow (step 2 of 2)**, then Home.
6. **An invite** (E1b) adds a Space screen first.

## What's wrong (against rules 1–2 and the reference)
1. **Two screens before the first input.** Both are mostly text. Create account and Sign in on E1 lead to the **same** email code, so the choice is pointless and confuses a first-time user.
2. **Too much reading.** A paragraph and 6 rotating cards on E1, and a body and terms on E2. A foreman with basic reading should see one line and one button.
3. **E9 asks six things at once.** That's the heaviest moment, right after the code.
4. **The pilot's real goal is to get into the site's Space, but onboarding ends at "People to follow".** Someone with an invite should land in their Space, on Work.
5. **There are no first-run tips** for the four actions that matter: sign as done, hold, swipe, the camera.
6. **The waiting moments** (sending the code, checking it, saving) need a clear line like "Checking your code…", not just a spinner.

## Proposal (onboarding v2): about 6 taps from the first open to the Space
1. **Welcome (one screen; replaces E1 and E2):**
   - language at the top (English · العربية);
   - a site picture (a proof photo with its DONE stamp and a pass);
   - one line: "Your site work, checked and on your record.";
   - **Continue with email** as the one main button;
   - "I have an invite code" as the second;
   - "Just looking" as a small link;
   - Google and Apple below, only when enabled.
2. **Email:** one field, then "Send code".
3. **Code:** the 6 boxes and "Checking…". "Try another way" offers a different email or resending.
4. **Your name:** first name and last name only.
5. **Your trade:** big tiles with icons, from the market pack (Engineer · Site supervisor · Foreman · Electrician · Plumber · Steel fixer · Carpenter · Mason · Supplier …), plus search and "Not listed".
   - Company, country and mobile leave onboarding. The country comes from the phone's region or the market and can be changed later in Profile; the company can be added later.
6. **Join your site:**
   - an invite code (6 characters) or an invite link that is already open, then "Join request sent", or straight in when invited;
   - "Skip for now" goes Home.
   - Someone who arrived through an invite skips this step and lands in that Space.
7. **The first time Work opens:** 3 short tips, one at a time, each pointing at the real control:
   - "Tap the camera to post a photo";
   - "Hold a card to share or delete";
   - "Sign as done when your work is finished".
   - Each shows once and can be dismissed.

**Moved to Home:** People to follow becomes a "People you may know" card in Home or Explore (polish queue items 3 and 5), not a step in onboarding.

**What doesn't change:** email code only (decision 04); no passwords; masked contact details; the server rules for markets.
