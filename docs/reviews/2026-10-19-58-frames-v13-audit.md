## A rule for every slice from now on
**Text sizes in frames drawn before 18 Oct are superseded by the two-size rule** (`BvType`, review 53 Part C). Build the layout and content from the frame, and the sizes from the tokens. A frame is redrawn only when its **layout** changes, that is where it still has the hour spine. Graphics keep their own sizes: stamps, the calendar leaf, the lock-screen clock.
