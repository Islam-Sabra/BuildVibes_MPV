## F3. Hold bar on a poll: votes count as acting
- Review 113's rule: Delete only while nobody has acted; otherwise Archive.
- **A vote is acting:** deleting would erase other people's answers. So once anyone other than the author has voted, the bar offers **Archive**, never Delete.
- Enforce it in SQL too: the delete function refuses a poll with other people's votes.
- **pgTAP test**, plus a widget test.
