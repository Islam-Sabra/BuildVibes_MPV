### S1 (before live): a follow-up that arrives after a new quiet time has started
- **The case:** Omar sends 1 message (a push), then 1 more 2 minutes later (a follow-up is queued for minute 5). At minute 5:20 he sends a third. The quiet time is over, so it gets its own push with its text.
- **The problem:** the cron sends the follow-up up to a minute after `not_before`, so it arrives after that push. It replaces it on the lock screen (same tag), says "2 new messages" and counts the third message, which was already pushed.
- **Fix in `claim_push`:**
  - If a `dm_message` push for the same person and conversation was queued at or after the follow-up's `not_before`, close the follow-up with no push (`error = 'superseded'`).
  - Count only the messages before `not_before`.
- **Test:** first push, a second message at +2 min, a third at +5:20. The follow-up is closed and only the third message's push goes.
