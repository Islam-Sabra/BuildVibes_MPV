## App, in build order
1. **Messages entry.**
   - A chat icon in the Home app bar opens Messages (space-scenarios). Its dot shows only when something is unread, never a number (rule 3).
   - Starting a thread with someone (`start_dm`): from + in DM1 and from S7-N.
   - On a person's profile: **Message** beside Follow. Checked 20 Oct in R5 v3 `2345:38825` and Omar's profile `2181:16328`. The old comment in `person_profile_screen.dart` ("Message is not in the MVP") goes. "Message about work" under "Open to" is Jobs and stays out.
2. **DM1 Messages list.**
   - The "Message request" row on top, then Today and Earlier.
   - An unread dot, and voice and file previews.
   - Search over names, and + to start one (the person picker).
3. **DM2 a thread.**
   - The header: name, then role and company. A typed company stays plain text (rule 4).
   - The strip "Both in <Space — Team>" (`dm_shared_space`).
   - Text, photo, file and voice bubbles, reusing the chat's widgets.
   - The composer: 📎, field and mic.
   - Live through the private `dm:` channel, and read marks (`mark_dm_read`).
4. **DM3 hold a message:** Reply, Copy, Put in a Space, Report. Save to my notebook comes with slice 8; leave it out, don't grey it.
5. **DM4–DM5 Put in a Space.** Pick a Space you're in. A work item opens there, writing in place, with the message quoted (`p_quote`: author, words, time). It never posts by itself (F56): only Post posts.
6. **DM6 a request.** The strip "Not in your Spaces · Decline · Block", and photos behind "Tap to show". The thread's ⋯ menu has Report person, Block and Mute (review 41).
7. **S7-N New chat in a Space.** A one-to-one starts from the Space's chat. The footer says it opens in Messages, "From Kuwait City Tower": the Space's name comes from the data, never typed.
8. **S8m Turn into a work item** in Space chat: the same writing card as W2 v4c, with "FROM CHAT" and the quote (`p_source_message`). A Team thread's message stays in that Team; the server already checks this.
