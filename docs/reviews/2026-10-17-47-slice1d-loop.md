## For the founder
**Proof photo: camera only, or the gallery too?** Today proof photos come only from the camera, at the moment of signing. That's what makes "proof" worth something on a profile: the time and place are real. A foreman who took photos during the day with his normal camera can't use them to sign later.
- **Recommendation:** camera only for proof during the pilot. The gallery stays allowed for new work items and updates. If the pilot team finds it blocks them, we add the gallery for proof with a visible mark ("from the gallery", with the photo's own date).
