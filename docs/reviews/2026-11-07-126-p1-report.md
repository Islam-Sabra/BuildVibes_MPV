## What was done
**One source:** where someone works now is their current Experience entry, the one with no end month. If there are several, the latest start wins. The basics' company columns are gone.

### SQL: migration `20261203120000_review126_current_work.sql`
- **`current_work(profile_id uuid)`** (for SQL):
  - Returns `{id, title, company_id, company_name_text, company_name}`, or null.
  - It shows the entry only to whoever may read it: the owner, or `experience_read`'s rule (not blocked; everyone, or connections when connected). It also never crosses a demo split.
  - So it never shows more than the profile already allowed.
- **The view `current_work`** (for the app):
  - The same, one row per person, with `security_invoker`.
  - The app embeds it from `profiles` the way it embedded `companies` before.
  - Why a view as well as the function:
    - App users have column-level grants on `profiles` (not `phone`, for one), so a function taking the whole `profiles` row is refused for them.
    - The view embeds by `profile_id` instead.
  - Checked on the local REST API as a signed-in user: from `profiles`, nested in `space_members`, and the search call.
- **`set_current_company(company_id, company_name_text)`:** what the basics and onboarding company field call now.
  - It always writes the caller's own entry.
  - With a current entry, its company changes and its title stays.
  - With none, a company makes one, titled with the headline, else the role (trade) in the profile's language, from this month.
- **`people_at_current_company(query, company_id)`:**
  - Search, by any part of the current company's name, registered or typed.
  - Near you, by your registered company.
  - Row-level security on `profiles` applies.
- **Readers moved to `current_work`:**
  - `company_team_preview` (Teams from companies);
  - `person_company` (and so `company_team_for`, the Team match);
  - `space_key_people` (key-role rows now carry `current_work`);
  - `dm_person` and the feed's `post_cards`. These two show the current title too, since the headline under a name is now the current title.
- **Back-fill:**
  - Every profile with a basics company and no current entry gets one: that company, titled with the headline, else the role, from the profile's creation month, visible to everyone (as the basics company was).
  - The rule lives in one function, `current_work_from_basics`, which `set_current_company` uses too, so the pgTAP test checks the same code the back-fill runs. App users can't call it.
  - A profile with neither a headline nor a role can't be titled, so it is skipped. The migration prints how many (see question 1).
- **Then the drop:** `profiles.company_id` and `company_name_text`, with their foreign key, checks, index, column grants and the demo-link trigger. Experience keeps its own demo-link trigger.
- **Rule 4 stays:** a typed name stays plain text. Only a company picked from the registered list is linked, and nothing creates a company.

### App
- **Selects:** every person select embeds `current_work(title, company_id, company_name)` in place of `company_name_text` and `companies(name)`.
  - Space lists, search, Near you, the profile, and my own profile.
  - Key-role rows read the same field from `space_key_people`.
- **One parser:** `CurrentWork` (`lib/data/models/current_work.dart`). `SpacePerson`, `MyProfile`, `ProfileView` and the suggestions all read it.
  - The title is the current title, else the headline, else the trade (as before).
  - The company is the current entry's, registered in ink, typed in plain grey.
- **Writes:** the basics (`saveBasics`) and onboarding (`saveAboutYou`) no longer write a company to `profiles`; they call `set_current_company`. The company field looks the same.
- **Search** finds people by their current company through `people_at_current_company`.
- **Near you** ("your company") uses the same call, with your registered company.

### Fakes and dev data

- `supabase/dev/seed_feed.sql` and `seed_key_people.sql` write Experience entries.
