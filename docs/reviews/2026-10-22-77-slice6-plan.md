## What already exists
- **Server (migration 20, live):**
  - `create_walk(space, parent, title, items[{id, line, owner, photo}], place, team)`:
    - a parent work item owned by its author, plus one work item per photo, each with its photo;
    - 1 to 30 photos, all or nothing;
    - idempotent by the parent's id;
    - one push per owner for the whole walk (`batch_pushes`).
  - `is_target`: a parent owned by its author with live work items under it.
    - Nobody is asked to sign it or check it, and it can't be signed as done.
    - So it never counts as proof.
  - The card JSON carries `children {total, checked, rows (first 10)}`. Each row has its title, state, owner and cover.
  - Slice 5's page lists the work items under one, with their stamps.
- **App:**
  - A tap on the camera starts the writing card with a photo; a hold signs as done (proof).
  - Photos go through `photo_privacy`: location, phone identity, re-encode (F60).
  - The work outbox sends posts and their photos when the signal is back.
  - Nothing yet for walks or target cards.

## App, in build order
1. **Walk mode (R1).**
   - **Where it starts:** "Walk" on the camera's mode switch (Photo · Walk). The camera opens on Photo, as today, and Walk is one tap.
   - **First the walk's name and place:** one line, for example "Walk, level 4 corridor", and the place, prefilled from the last place used in this Space.
   - **Then, for each photo:**
     - take it;
     - write its line ("Hanger missing above door 4.12", 2–140 characters);
     - give it an owner. The chip opens the give-to picker; the last owner is kept for the next photo.
     - The thumbnails grow; a tap on one opens it to change its line or owner, or to take it out.
   - **Finish** posts the whole walk through the outbox:
     1. the photos are uploaded first, each into its work item's folder;
     2. then one `create_walk` call.
     - With no signal, it waits and sends later, as other posts do.
   - **✕** asks before throwing the walk away, once there is a photo. The walk is kept on the phone until it is sent, so a killed app comes back to it.
   - **At most 30 photos**, as the server allows. At 30, the shutter says so.
2. **The target card (R1b, N5), in the daybook:**
   - one card for the walk: its title, "4 photos, each one a work item" (a fact about this post), the progress line, the photos in a row, and one row per work item (a round state, the words, a stamp when Done or Checked, the owner's face), then "N of M checked";
   - more than 10 rows: the first 10, then "Show all" opens the page (slice 5);
   - a row's tap opens that work item as its own card (see question 1);
   - the parent shows no Sign as done and no paper clip, because the server never asks anyone to sign a target.
3. **Work items under one, everywhere else:**
   - the page's list (slice 5) stays as it is;
   - Needs you and Activity open the child's own card;
   - the owner signs as done from the child, as any work item.

## Questions for the lead
1. **Do a walk's work items also show as their own cards in the daybook?**
   - Today `daybook_cards` returns every work item, so a 10-photo walk is 11 cards, the parent and 10 children. R1b draws one card.
   - **Recommendation: fold them into the target card.**
     - Migration 37 makes `daybook_cards` leave out a work item whose parent is a live target the reader can see.
     - It gives each child row its own `needs_me`, so the owner sees the paper clip and "Sign as done" on their row.
     - A push, Needs you or Activity opens the parent's card with that row highlighted.
   - The other way, with no migration: children stay as their own cards, and the target card lists them too. That's simpler, but the daybook fills up on a long walk.
2. **Voice for a photo's line:** the mic in R1 types with the phone keyboard's own dictation, with no audio kept and no new package. **Recommendation: yes.** A voice note per photo would need a server change to `create_walk`, and the work item's title must be words anyway.
3. **Camera:** the app takes photos with the phone's own camera (`image_picker`), so walk mode is shoot, back to the line, shoot again: one tap more per photo than a live viewfinder. A live viewfinder needs the `camera` package and a second photo path through `photo_privacy`.
   - **Recommendation: keep `image_picker` for the pilot.** It's the path F60 was proven on, on the founder's Xiaomi.

## Rules to keep
- Arabic mirrored, with the glossary's words, Almarai 16/13, Western digits.
- No counts as quality signals. "4 photos" and "N of M checked" are facts about this walk.
- Photos lose their location and the phone's identity (`photo_privacy`), as every photo does.
- Every permission is the server's: who may post a walk, who owns a work item, and that a target is never signed.
