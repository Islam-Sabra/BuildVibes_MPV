# Review 120: one-day call test (Agora) before building calls

**From:** the lead, 4 Nov 2026.

**The founder's decisions (4 Nov):**
- Voice and video calls go in before the pilot, **one to one only**:
  - **Inside a Space** (a member, a key person, the Space chat): **App call** (Agora, voice or video) or **Phone call**.
  - **Outside a Space** (a direct message, a profile): **Phone call only**, through the phone's own dialler, never an app call (founder, 4 Nov).
  - A number shows only by its owner's choice, through **one single-choice control** (rule 6) on the profile, "Who sees my number" «من يرى رقمي»:
    - Nobody «لا أحد» (the default);
    - Members of my Spaces «أعضاء مساحاتي»;
    - My Spaces and people I message «مساحاتي ومن أراسلهم».
  - With no number to show, a direct message has no Call button.
- Agora for the pilot.
- Each call offers **App call** or **Phone call** (the phone number shows only by the person's choice).
- A server cap keeps us inside the free 10,000 minutes a month.
- The full design comes in a later review, together with key people and the chart.

**This review is a throwaway test.** It answers three questions before we build:
1. Do Agora calls work well on Kuwait mobile networks?
2. Can a call ring on the founder's Xiaomi when the app is closed?
3. How much does it add to the app's size and battery use?

## Rules for the test
- **Branch:** `claude/call-spike` off `slice2-app`. **It is never merged.** The findings go in a report; the code is thrown away.
- **Separate app:** application id suffix `.spike`, named "BV call test". It installs **next to** build 1079 and doesn't replace it. It is uninstalled at the end.
- **Agora:**
  - The founder creates a free Agora account and a project in **Testing mode (App ID only)**. No card.
  - He puts the App ID in `env/dev.json` himself, as `AGORA_APP_ID`. Never commit it, never paste it in chat.
  - After the test he deletes that Agora project or switches it to Secured mode.
- **Phone:** confirm 22120RN86G, install only "BV call test", disconnect afterwards. Nothing goes live and nothing touches Supabase.

## What to build (minimal)
- Use `agora_rtc_engine`. First check that its latest version **builds on Flutter 3.44**. If it doesn't, report that and stop. Remember Phosphor.
- One debug screen:
  - a channel name, **Join voice** and **Join video (HD 720p)**;
  - mute, speaker, camera switch, Leave;
  - and live stats from `onRtcStats` and `onNetworkQuality`: round trip in ms, packet loss %, quality 1–6, and the bitrate.
- **Ringing test, part 2:** use `flutter_callkit_incoming` (or the plain Android route: a full-screen intent and a call-style notification), triggered by an FCM high-priority data message sent by hand from the PC.
  - Check whether it rings full screen with the app **swiped away** and the **screen locked** on the Xiaomi:
    - with Autostart on;
    - with Autostart off.

## The lead's note on the first results (5 Nov)
Read from Cursor's summary of `claude/call-spike` at b4f4ba1f. The full check waits until the last rows are done.

- **Quality on mobile data:**
  - connects in about 0.5 s, no packet loss;
  - 0.45–0.8 s end to end, with the PC emulator at one end;
  - good enough so far.
- **Size is the new risk:**
  - Agora's full SDK takes the download from about 14 MB to 58 MB;
  - the trimmed estimate is about 38 MB, and that is only worked out, not built.
  - On cheap site phones with little storage, that matters (rule 2). The size target for the pilot: **the download grows by no more than 15 MB**.
- **Added to the remaining test:**
  1. **Build the trimmed Agora**: voice and video only, with no extensions (AI noise suppression, beauty, content inspection, spatial audio, etc.). Measure the real arm64 download and installed size.
  2. **For comparison, a size-only build with `livekit_client`** (WebRTC), no calls. Measure the same two numbers. It needs no account.
  3. Then the pending rows:
     - a call over the company Wi-Fi;
     - 10 minutes of battery;
     - ringing, with Autostart on and off.
- **Decision rule:**
  - if trimmed Agora fits the 15 MB target, we stay with Agora;
  - if it doesn't and LiveKit does, the lead re-plans the provider before the build review. The app's call screens stay the same either way.
- **After the test:** delete `fcm-spike.json` and its key in the Firebase console, and delete the Agora test project or reset its certificate.

## Size target revised (founder, 5 Nov)
- **The founder:** most workers have cheap Samsung phones with at least 6 GB of RAM, so phone power isn't the limit.
- **The size target is now: download no more than about 40 MB**, close to WhatsApp's size.
- **Trimmed Agora (about 38 MB, estimated) is expected to fit.** Build and measure it in the remaining test.
- **The LiveKit size-only build is dropped.** Only if trimmed Agora comes out above 40 MB does the lead re-plan.
- **Still required:** leave out every SDK extension a call doesn't need. Those megabytes buy nothing.
