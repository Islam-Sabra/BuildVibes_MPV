## What already exists
- **Server (migration 20, live; migration 30, not live):**
  - `post_templates`: a built-in (no Space) or a Space's own. Four kinds: work item, checklist, announcement, report. A template holds words only: blocks (text, headings, toggles, tables) and `fields {title, body, points}`.
  - `save_template(id, space, from_post, name)`:
    - a Lead or a Space admin;
    - at most 50 in a Space;
    - a name of 1–60 characters;
    - keeps the post's Team, so a Team's template shows only to that Team and the admins;
    - idempotent by id.
  - `delete_template(id)`: a Lead or a Space admin; built-ins stay.
  - `space_templates(space)`: the built-ins first, then the Space's own, newest first.
  - `create_space_post` and `create_work_item` take `p_template` (the same kind and audience) and `p_blocks`; `create_work_item` also takes `p_points`.
  - The built-in **Daily report** (`builtin_key = daily_report`): three headings by key, `on_site`, `done_today` and `problems`. The app gives each its words in each language.
  - `daily_report_fill(space, team, from, to)`:
    - what was checked that day: title, ref, owner, checker, the Done photo;
    - only work that everyone who reads the report may see;
    - one day at a time, at most 100.
  - Migration 30 (not live): `daily_report_notes(...)` gives the day's notes for "On site", with the same rules. An `item_ref` may point at a note.
  - The post JSON has `refs`: each `item_ref` as this reader may see it.
- **App:**
  - slice 5's page (blocks, /, tables, saving with a version);
  - `PageBlock.Other` keeps an `item_ref` it doesn't draw;
  - `DaybookPost.isShown` leaves out kind `report`.

  - `share_plus` is in the app. `pdfx` only shows PDFs; it doesn't make them.

## App, in build order
1. **Templates when writing (daybook.md §More: "Template").**
   - **Where:** "Template" in the writing card's More row, for a work item, checklist or announcement. Hidden when the Space has no template of that kind (review 44).
   - **The picker:** a sheet listing this kind's templates, each by its name. A built-in shows its name in the reader's language, and the Space's own templates follow, newest first. There is no count and no "most used" (rule 3).
   - **Choosing one fills the card:** the title and words, the points for a checklist, and the page. The card shows one quiet line, "From: Concrete pour check". The person still changes anything before posting. The post goes with `p_template` and `p_blocks`.
   - **A Team's template:** choosing it sets the post's audience to that Team. If the audience is set first, only that Team's templates and the Everyone ones are listed, so the server never refuses.
   - **Templates are words only**, as the server keeps them: no photos, files, people, owner, due date or work items under one.
2. **Save as template.**
   - "Save as template" in a post's menu (F16), for a Lead or a Space admin, on a work item, checklist, announcement or report.
   - It asks for a name (1–60), prefilled with the post's title.
   - At 50, the sheet says the Space has its 50 and offers Space info › Templates.
3. **The Space's templates (Space info › Templates).**
   - For a Lead or a Space admin: the Space's own templates, each with its kind and its Team; Delete asks first.
   - Built-ins are listed but can't be deleted.
   - Others see nothing here: they use templates from the writing card.
4. **The daily report (R2, redrawn).**
   - **Where it starts:** "Daily report" in the Space menu ⋯, for a Lead or Crew. It opens the writing card as kind `report`, from the built-in Daily report.
   - **The title:** the day, for example "Thursday 15 October", in the reader's language, with Western digits. The person can change it.
   - **The audience:** Everyone, or one Team (F56's one choice). The fill follows it.
   - **"Done today" fills itself:** `daily_report_fill` for the phone's day, from 00:00 to now. Each checked work item goes in as an `item_ref`. It is drawn as one line, "Sleeves at C7, checked 10:12", with its stamp, and a tap opens its card.
   - **"On site" suggests the day's notes** (F58, migration 30): each one has "Add". Without migration 30 live, "On site" is a plain heading to write under.
   - **"Problems"** is for the person to write.
   - **Photos:** the Done photos of the work items it brings in, as a row (R2). Photos taken for the report go in as any post's photos.
   - **Before posting:** the page can be changed as in slice 5, and an `item_ref` can be taken out. Posting sends one `create_space_post(kind: report, template, blocks)` through the outbox.
   - **One report per day?** The server allows more than one. The app opens today's own draft if there is one, and otherwise starts a new report. A second posted report is allowed (for example, one per Team).
5. **The report card in the daybook.**
   - `isShown` takes kind `report`.
   - The card: the header, the day, the three headings with their lines, the photos, and **Share as PDF**.
   - Readers see only the `refs` they may read. A ref the reader can't see is left out, never shown as a grey row.
   - A report is not a work item: no stamps of its own, no Sign as done, and it never counts as proof.
6. **Share as PDF.**
   - Made on the phone (data-model §17) with the `pdf` package, then the share sheet (`share_plus`).
   - The PDF holds the Space's name, the day, the author, the three headings and their lines, the refs with their stamps and checker, and the photos. Photos go through `photo_privacy` first (F60), as every photo that leaves the phone.
   - **Fonts:** the bundled Plus Jakarta Sans and Almarai, embedded. An Arabic report is right to left.
   - No logo and no company facts (rule 4). No country words: dates come from the reader's locale and the market pack (rule 5).
   - This is not "export a work item's record as PDF" (out of the MVP). Only the daily report has it.
