## Part 1. Screen layout (SP1)
- **The Space's bottom tab bar goes.** The four tabs become:
  - a **Work · Chat** switch under the header, with Chat showing its unread count;
  - **Library** (folder icon) and **People** (people icon) in the header, beside Search.
- **People carries a dot** when join requests are waiting (Needs you), so an admin doesn't miss them.
- **⋯ goes from the header.** Tapping the Space's name opens Space info, which holds what ⋯ held.
- **The post bar is the bottom of the screen:** "Write in this Space" plus the camera. The height the tab bar used goes to the work.
- **Arabic:** the whole header mirrors, the back arrow points right, and the switch starts with Work at the right.

## Part 2. One product language (as review 100 Part 0)
- **Tap** opens. **Hold** selects, then the bottom bar shows Share · Duplicate · Delete (SP6).
  - There is no Move, because work stays in its Space (rule 8).
  - Delete isn't offered for a Checked item.
- **Swipe toward the end:** Pin · Reminder (SP5). Both are mine only:
  - pins are per person (as now);
  - a reminder is a phone notification for me.
- **Swipe toward the start:** Archive, **only for a Lead and only on a Checked item.** It's a round action the same size as Pin and Reminder, never a wide block (founder, 21:49).
  - It hides the item from Work for everyone; Search and the Library still find it.
  - On any other item, swiping toward the start does nothing.
- **Signatures are never on a swipe or a hold.** Sign as done, Check and Send back stay deliberate taps.

## Part 3. The writing card (SP2)
- **The kind chips are one single choice, scrolling sideways:** Note · Work item · Checklist · Announcement · Scheduled.
  - They use the Space's words and icons.
  - **Work item is the default in a Space.**
- **Title, then details.** The hint names `+` for bullet points, a table or a table of tasks.
- **For · Place · Due · Team are rows that open pickers,** as review 104 N10:
  - For uses the Give to picker; a work item takes exactly one person.
  - Place is a site tag (Part 5).
  - Team defaults to "Whole Space".
  - A row shows only for the kinds that use it (Note has no For or Due).
- **The bar is the notebook's:** `+ · 📷 · 📎 · 🎤`, then Send.

## Part 4. Shapes of a work item (SP3, SP4) — corrected 28 Oct 21:49
- `+` inside a work item offers Text · Bullet points · Checklist · Table · **Table of tasks** · Photo · File or scan.
- **Table of tasks** (founder: one owner, not one per row):
  - **The work item keeps its one owner.** The rows are tasks in a table: a tick, Task, Note.
  - **The owner** (or a Lead) ticks each row. A tick is not a signature.
  - The For line shows the progress: "1 of 3 ticked".
  - **"Sign as done" happens once, for the whole item, with one proof photo,** as for any work item. It is offered at any time, not only when every row is ticked.
  - A ticked row is crossed out. "Add a row" adds a row.
  - It is stored as a table block with a tick column inside the work item. **It is not work items under one work item.**
- **Table** (plain) is the existing table block, with no ticks.
- **Bullet points** are the existing text blocks with a bullet.
- **Arabic:** the columns mirror, with the tick at the right.

## Part 5. Site tags (SP1–SP3)
- **Each Space keeps a short list of places:** levels, zones, grids ("L3 · Grid B4").
  - A Lead adds places from Space info, and anyone who may post can add one from the Place picker.
- **A post or work item has one place.** It shows as a sand tag with a pin.
  - Tapping the tag filters Work to that place.
  - Search finds places.
- **Not free text any more:** a list keeps "L3 B4" and "Level 3 grid B4" from becoming two places.
- The proof photo's place label uses the tag.

## Part 6. Needs you line and morning brief (SP1)
- **One line at the top of Work:** "3 need you · 2 due today ›".
  - Tapping it filters Work to items waiting on me.
  - It shows only when there's something waiting. There are no charts or percentages.
- **Morning brief:** a pinned announcement stays at the top until the day ends (Space time zone).
  - Members tap **Got it** (this already exists as an acknowledgement).
  - The Lead sees the faces and names: "Omar, Rakesh and 4 more".

## Part 7. Before / after and the proof moment (SP7)
- **Before** is the photo posted with the work item. **After** is the Sign as done photo.
  - When both exist, they show side by side with their times, and the Checked stamp sits on After.
  - No new upload.
- **The proof moment:** when an item is Checked, its owner sees a card for 4 s:
  - "Added to your proof of work · Checked by Islam Sabra · 11:20 · View";
  - View opens the proof on their profile.
  - It shows once, on the owner's phone only.

## Part 8. People (SP8)
- **Each row shows:**
  - name;
  - title · company. The title is the profile's headline, otherwise the trade.
  - pass (Lead / Crew / Viewer) and team.
- **Company names:** a registered company is ink medium; a typed company stays plain grey text (rule 4).
- **This is mostly built already.** `people_tab.dart` already shows title · company. Where the founder saw blank lines, the profiles had no headline, trade or company. **Check with the pilot team's real profiles.**
- **Join requests sit on top** under "Needs you", with Decline · Confirm. Confirm asks for the pass, as now.

## Part 9. Removing someone with open work (SP9)
- **Removing a member who owns open work items** opens a sheet listing each item with a "Give to" picker.
  - The default is the person who created the item.
- **"Give and remove" does both in one step on the server.** It fails if any item is still without a new owner.
- **Their Checked work stays** in the Space and on their profile.
- **Leaving by themselves** shows the same sheet.
