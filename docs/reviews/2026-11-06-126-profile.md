# Review 126: Profile, the founder's notes (started 6 Nov)

## P1. Your company comes from your Experience, one source only
- **Seen on build 1103:**
  - the founder's Experience has **IMAR Group · BIM Manager · Present**, but the Space shows him as "Project manager" with **no company**;
  - another member shows "Project manager · GCG".
- **Cause (live data, read-only):** there are **two places** for "where I work now":
  - `profiles.company_id / company_name_text`, a field in the profile basics. The Space rows and Teams from companies read only this one;
  - the Experience entry with no end date ("Present").
  - The founder filled in only Experience, so he has no company in the Space, and **Teams from companies can't see him.**
- **The fix: one source, Experience.**
  - **Current company** = the Experience entry with no end date. If there are several, the one that started most recently. Its title is the **current job title** («Present»).
  - **The basics' company field goes away.** Entering a company in the basics or onboarding **creates or updates the current Experience entry** instead. There's one place to type it.
  - **Everything reads the current Experience entry:**
    - Space member rows;
    - search;
    - Near you;
    - Teams from companies (`company_team_preview`);
    - key-role rows;
    - the profile header.
  - **The title under the name (the headline):** the current Experience title. If there's no Experience, the trade or role from onboarding, as now.
  - **Rule 4 stays:** a typed company name remains plain text, unless it matches a registered company.
  - **Migration:** for every profile that has a basics company but no current Experience entry, **create one** (company, title = headline or role, start = the profile's creation month), then drop the basics column. A view or function `current_work(profile)` serves everyone.
- **Tests:**
  - pgTAP: a current entry drives the Space row, Teams from companies and search; ending it (setting an end month) removes the company everywhere; the backfill migration keeps GCG for the existing member.
  - Widget: profile header and member row, in EN and AR.
- **Status:** written from the founder's phone check. He hasn't started his Profile notes yet, so more comes in this review.

## P3. Project studio (founder, 7 Nov)
One studio for **any** kind of project: building, infrastructure, interior, design study, software, website, plugin or tool, research, course, other.
- **Simple (default, PS1):** media already picked from the project's Space (or camera/gallery) → a **drawing title block** already filled: Name · What it is · Status · My role · With · Dates → Add to my profile. Same dark studio as Explore.
- **Advanced (PS2):** cover, order, Before · After, stamps (by kind and stage: FIRST POUR, TOPPING OUT, HANDOVER; IFC ISSUED; V1 RELEASED…), captions; extras through dashed chips: Location · Client · Link · Stage (Design · Tender · Construction · Handover) · Tools used · Teammates · What I did; Who sees it (Everyone · Team only · Hidden). Draft saves itself; everything can be added later from Edit.
- **Opened (PS3):** media, the title block with only the fields it has, what they did.
- **Input methods (typing only for the name search, a link, and "What I did"):** name = search the shared records; What it is · Stage · Status · Who sees it = one-choice lists; role = your Experience titles, then the market-pack role list; With · Client = your Experience companies, then registered companies (typed stays plain); dates = month/year + Now, prefilled from the Space join date or the record; location = market-pack places + optional pin; tools = market-pack chips + own; teammates = people search (one notification); link = paste, type detected.
- **Many companies:** the shared record lists every company with its part (Client · Consultant · Main contractor · Subcontractor · Supplier, from the market pack; approved PJ1). "With" is the person's own company; **a joint venture takes up to 2** (founder, 7 Nov).
- **Site signboard from the record** (print PDF with a QR to the record; legal fields from the market pack): **after the pilot, only from the company page of the project's main contractor** (founder, 7 Nov).
- **Data (for the build prompt):** `cv_projects` gains media (ordered, cover), `what_i_did`, `company` (from Experience or registered, up to 2), `featured` + order, `visibility`; `projects` gains `kind` and `stage` (and drops the building-only assumption: location optional). Space photos: only the person's own photos, copied on choice (rule 8).
