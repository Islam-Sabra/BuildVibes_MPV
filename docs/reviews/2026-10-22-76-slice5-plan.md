## What already exists
- **Server (migration 20, live):**
  - `space_posts.blocks` and `blocks_version`;
  - `validate_blocks`: text, heading (level 1 or 2), toggle (holding text, heading, table, photo, file), table (1–20 rows × 1–8 cells), photo and file (the post's own `post_files`), `checklist_ref` (point ids of this post, each in one block), `child_item` (a work item under this one); at most 200 blocks and 100 KB; no page on a file post;
  - `save_blocks(id, blocks, base_version, add_files)`: the author only, while the post can be edited (a work item only while Open); a stale version is refused with 40001; a photo and its block save together; one "Page edited" line; the owner is told at most once in 10 minutes;
  - `post_page(id)`: the full post, with `blocks`, `blocks_version`, `files`, `under` (its work items, with stamp, owner and cover) and `parent`;
  - `create_work_item(…, p_parent)`: a work item under a top-level work item; `edit_space_post` adds, rewords, reorders and removes points (a ticked one can't be removed or reworded).
- **App:** nothing reads or writes `blocks` yet. A card's history opens on the card itself; there is no full page.

## Server: one small change, migration 36 (35 is the 3D notes)
**Turn into, in one step.** Turning a checklist point into a work item under this one takes three calls today:
1. `create_work_item(p_parent)`;
2. `save_blocks`, swapping the point's block for a `child_item`;
3. `edit_space_post`, removing the point.

Losing the signal halfway leaves a point and its work item both on the page. So:
- `turn_point_into_item(p_post, p_point, p_item_id, p_owner, p_base_version)` does all three in one transaction:
  - the author only, while editable;
  - the point not ticked;
  - idempotent by `p_item_id`, so a retry is the same item.
- It returns the new `blocks_version`.
- Turning a line of text into a point (and back) needs no new function: it's an `edit_space_post` for the points plus a `save_blocks`. Two calls, made in that order, so a failure leaves at worst a point that no block shows. The page shows such points at the end, so nothing is lost.
- Tests:
  - a non-member, a Viewer, the owner (not the author) and anon can't do it;
  - a ticked point is refused;
  - a retry gives the same item;
  - a stale version gives 40001.
- The same routine as migrations 29–34: an independent check, then a push with the founder's yes, as the only file.

**If the lead prefers no migration:** the app makes the three calls in the order above and, after a failure, finishes on the next open (the item id is the app's own). It works, but it's more app code to get right than about 60 lines of SQL.

## App, in build order
1. **The page (N1).** A tap on a card (not on its action, photo or stamp) opens its page as a sheet rising over the daybook, the same paper, with a torn top edge:
   - the head: avatar, "You · 08:14 · Kuwait City Tower", and ✕;
   - the title, the facts line (owner, due, place), the text;
   - then the blocks in order:
     - **toggle:** closed, opens in place;
     - **checklist points:** ticked in place, for whoever may tick;
     - **table:** scrolls sideways past 3 columns;
     - **photo:** opens the viewer;
     - **file:** opens F1;
     - **work items under this one:** each row with its stamp and owner's face, and "1 of 2 checked" (a fact about this item, not a quality signal).
   - Below: the card's history and its one action, as on the card.
   - Read from `post_page`, kept for the next open with no signal.
   - Every kind but a file post has a page; a note's page is its text and blocks.
2. **Edit the page (N2), the author only, while the server allows it:**
   - "Edit page" in the card's long-press menu and on the page's head; then ⋮⋮ on each block, **Done** instead of ✕, and "+ Type / to add a block" at the end.
   - Words are edited in place.
   - A long press on ⋮⋮ drags a block, or a whole toggle with what's inside it.
   - Swiping a block toward the start offers Delete, with Undo.
   - Everyone else sees the page read-only, Viewers included.
3. **Add blocks (N3):** + or typing "/" at the start of an empty line opens the block menu: Text, Heading, Checklist, Toggle, Table, Photo (camera or gallery, through `photo_privacy`), File (the file chooser), and Work item under this one (title and owner in place, through the give-to picker).
   - Typing after "/" filters the menu.
   - The page keeps the 10-file limit (photos and files are the post's own).
   - A checklist adds its points to the post through `edit_space_post`.
4. **Turn into (N4):** the menu on ⋮⋮ offers Text ↔ Checklist point ↔ Work item under this one, the current one ticked.
   - A ticked point can't change, and the menu says so in one line.
   - A work item under one is never turned back: it may already have an owner's work on it.
5. **Tables:**
   - a 2 × 2 table to start;
   - + row and + column at the edges, up to 20 × 8;
   - the first row is the header, with a subtle fill;
   - a cell holds up to 200 characters, and a long press on a cell offers Delete row and Delete column.
6. **Saving:**
   - **Done** saves through `save_blocks` with the version it read.
   - A 40001 (the same author on another phone) reloads the page and applies the edit again where it can. If it can't, the author keeps their copy and is told "This page changed on another phone. Yours is kept here.", with Use mine or Use the newer one.
   - With no signal, the edit waits as the page's draft on the phone ("Waiting to send"), as posts do, and goes when the signal is back.
   - The owner is told by the server at most once in 10 minutes.
7. **The card:** a post whose page holds more than the card shows gets one quiet grey line under its text, "Open the page ›" / «افتح الصفحة ›», with no count of blocks.

## Rules to keep
- Arabic for every screen: mirrored, the glossary's words, Almarai at 16/13, Western digits; a table's columns run right to left.
- No counts as quality signals. "1 of 2 checked" counts this item's own work items, as the target does in N5.
- Only the author edits a page; the server enforces it (`save_blocks`, migration 36). Others read, and tick where they may.
- Every interaction is a single choice where the choices exclude each other (the block menu, Turn into).
- No generic screens: the page is the same paper as the card, with the paper clip, stamps and torn edge.
