## B. A refused reminder, and the message for it (comment 3)
- **Live log:** `set_work_reminder` returned **403** twice (08:24:40 and 08:24:49 UTC), so a `42501` came from the function.
- **Checked live as the founder:** on every open, live work item in NBKCH he is a member and a Lead, he can see the item, and he's the creator of most. Every rule in the function passes.
- **So the swiped card is the likely cause:** the sheet offers Someone on a card the server doesn't take, such as a checklist, an announcement, a sub-item or a table-of-tasks row. The server refuses anything that isn't a `work_item` post.
- **Debug first:** reproduce on the emulator, find which card and which check refused it, and fix the root cause. If the card is one the server rightly refuses, the sheet must not offer Someone there.
- **Also:** a refusal must say why, in plain words, never only "You can't do that in this Space". For the reminder:
  - "Only the person who made it, the person it's for, or a Lead can remind someone." («يذكّر الآخرين صاحب البند أو المكلّف أو المسؤول فقط.»)
  - "Reminders are only for open work items." («التذكير للبنود المفتوحة فقط.»)
  - The app maps each server message to its line; the generic line is only for the unknown.

## D. The composer, simple by default (comments 4 and 5) — PROPOSAL
**The problem in the founder's screenshots:** with the keyboard up, the Space header, Work · Chat and the date line stay on screen. The kind chips and the For, Place, Due and Team rows fill what's left. The title being typed scrolls out of view.

**The rule:** simple by default, deep only if the person asks.

| Frame | What | EN | AR |
|---|---|---|---|
| CP1 | Write: the composer takes the whole screen | `2707:39361` | `2707:39702` |
| CP2 | Tap **#**: the kinds as a grid, in place of the keyboard | `2707:39412` | `2707:39753` |
| CP3 | Tap **+**: the extras as a grid | `2707:39502` | `2707:39843` |
| CP4 | With extras: small chips with ×, and photos | `2707:39600` | `2707:39941` |
| CP5 | Hold **Send**: Post later | `2708:39511` | `2708:39561` |

- **Opening:** tapping "Write in this Space" opens the composer **over the whole screen**.
  - The Space header, Work · Chat, the date line and the feed are hidden while writing.
  - Its top bar has **×**, then "New work item" (the kind's name) with the Space's name under it, and **Drafts**.
- **The writing area:**
  - **Title**;
  - **Details (optional)**;
  - the **For** chip, for work items and checklists only: "For · Choose", or the person.
  - Nothing else shows until it's asked for.
- **One bar above the keyboard:** **#** (the kind, showing its icon) · **+** · **camera** · then **Send**. The paperclip moves into **+**.
- **Post later (CP5):** **hold Send** to get a small menu, **Post later…**, which opens review 108's Post later sheet. **The clock icon is removed from every writing bar** (the Space, the Feed and a notebook share), so nothing with a clock sits next to Due. Review 108 V7's "can't be scheduled yet" line moves to this menu.
- **# (CP2):** the keyboard gives way to a grid of the kinds:
  - Note · Work item · Checklist · Announcement · Scheduled activity · Check-in (after review 109) · Walk.
  - Tapping one picks it and brings the keyboard back.
  - The kind chips row is removed.
- **+ (CP3):** the keyboard gives way to a grid:
  - Place · Due · Team · Photos · File · Bullets · Table · Table of tasks.
  - Each item opens its own picker, as today.
  - Only the extras that make sense for the kind show (a Note has no Due).
- **What was added (CP4):** each added extra shows as a small chip under the details, with **×**: the place, the due time, a team other than the whole Space.
  - Photos show as thumbnails above the chips.
  - **Team** isn't shown when it's the whole Space, the default.
- **No other changes:** drafts and Send's rules stay as they are. The kind name shows once only, in the top bar title, not again above the title.
