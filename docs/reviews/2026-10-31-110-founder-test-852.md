## F. The panels, redrawn, and the short card (founder, 13:35–13:37)
**The panels (CP2 and CP3, final, founder 13:42–13:44): a grid, small and aligned.** It looks like a phone's attach menu, with no boxes and no list rows.
- Each item is a **46 px round icon** with its name under it (up to 2 lines, centred). The chosen one is a filled brand circle with white icon.
- **# (kinds):** 4 to a row, so 7 items fill a row of 4 and a row of 3:
  - Note · Work item · Announcement · Scheduled activity · Check-in · Thanks · Walk.
- **+ (extras for a work item):** 5 to a row, so 9 items fill a row of 5 and a row of 4:
  - Place · Due · Tag · Photos · File · Checklist · Bullets · Table · Table of tasks.
- **Arabic** mirrors English: rows start from the right.
- **Size follows the phone (founder, 13:48):** there's no size button. The circles and names scale with the phone's text-size setting (`MediaQuery.textScaler`), as the rest of the app does. At the largest phone size, the grid drops to 3 to a row (#) and 4 to a row (+), so names never break mid-word.

**Checklist is merged into the work item (founder, 13:42).** It is no longer a kind. A work item gets ticks through **+ → Checklist**, and For stays one person, the owner who signs. The existing checklist posts keep working and show as they are.

**The short card in the Space (HV1)**, the same rule for every kind:

| Part | Limit |
|---|---|
| Who · ref · time | 1 line |
| Title | 2 lines, then … |
| Details | 2 lines, then … |
| Info | 1 line: For · due (only what exists) |
| Tags | the place and the one tag, on 1 line |
| Photos | up to 3, then a "+N" tile |
| The rest | 1 quiet line: "2 files · 1 table · 6 tasks", with **See more** at its end |

- **A work item with a checklist inside** shows its first 3 lines, then "4 of 9 ticked" with See more.
- **A Thanks** is short by nature, so no See more is needed.
- A card is never more than about half the screen.

**See more (HV2)** opens the whole post in place, exactly like opening a post today:
- full details;
- all photos;
- the files;
- the table and the table of tasks;
- the info lines;
- the sign-off strip at the bottom.
