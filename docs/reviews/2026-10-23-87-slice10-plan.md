## What is still missing today
**No signal.** The outbox already keeps sign-offs, new posts (every kind), walks, checks and cancels on the phone and sends them when the signal is back. The notebook keeps its notes the same way (slice 8). What doesn't wait:
- **Send back, Give to and an update** (a card's long-press actions) say "No signal" and are lost.
- **A tick on a point, Got it, Received, On my way** show at once and are taken back with "No signal".
- **A page being edited** shows "No signal" on Done; leaving the editor loses the changes.
- **Meeting notes** keep their ticks on the screen and say "No signal. Try again." (accepted for the pilot, review 86 Q2).
- **Nothing says the phone has no signal.** OF's strip isn't built.

**Notifications.**
- **Six Activity kinds the server writes are never shown:** a change to something that is for you (`item_edited`), a cancel (`item_cancelled`), a new announcement, a checklist for you, an invitation to a scheduled activity, and a new version of a file you received. The app drops rows it doesn't know. `activity_rows()` also gives no title or post for them (it reads only `work_items`).
- **Their pushes open the Space,** not the post ("until the app has a route for one post"). The app now opens any post at its card (`?card=`).
- **F96:** a phone that fails to get a push token gives up after five tries until the next start.
- **F97:** a direct message inside another's 5-minute quiet time is never pushed.
- **Remind me** (review 83 Q2): `remind_at` is kept on the server, but nothing on the phone reminds.

**F98:** a new conversation starts with an empty screen above the first message.

## What the server already has
- `push_queue.not_before` and a cron every minute (`retry_push_queue`) that sends rows whose wait is over (migration 14). A row can be queued now to go later.
- `dm_members.last_read_at` (migration 22): what the reader has seen.
- `notifications.space_post_id` (migration 19) for the six kinds above; the push data carries `space_post_id`.
- `notes.remind_at` (migration 21), kept for a reinstall.

## Migration 39 (`20261031120000_slice10_notifications.sql`, not live)
1. **F97, one follow-up push per quiet time.**
   - When a direct message falls inside the quiet time (a `dm_message` push for that conversation in the last 5 minutes), `handle_dm_push` queues one `dm_follow_up` row, `not_before` the end of that quiet time, unless one is already waiting for it.
   - `claim_push` sends a `dm_follow_up` only if messages from that person after the first push are still unread then (`last_read_at`); it puts how many in the row (`count`). Read in time: it is closed and nothing is sent.
   - Never with the text; muted, declined, blocked and hidden accounts as today.
2. **Activity rows for the six kinds:** `activity_rows()` gives a daybook post's title and its id (in `work_item_id`, the card the row opens), and only while the reader may still see the post.
3. **Tests** in `supabase/tests/39_slice10_notifications.test.sql`, run on the stand-in.

**Edge function `push` (not deployed):** `dm_follow_up` reads "Omar Haddad · 2 new messages" / «عمر حداد · رسالتان جديدتان» and opens the conversation, replacing its earlier push (same tag); the six daybook-post kinds open the post at its card.

## App, in build order
1. **No signal (OF).**
   - The strip under the masthead, "No signal. Everything you post waits and sends by itself.", while the last try to reach the server failed for want of a signal; it goes as soon as something gets through.
   - **Send back, Give to and an update** go through the outbox: shown at once ("Waiting to send" on the card), sent when the signal is back, refusals said as today.
   - **Ticks and Got it / Received / On my way** stay on the card with no signal and are sent again when the signal is back, instead of being taken back.
   - **A page being edited:** with no signal, Done keeps the editor open with "No signal. Your changes stay here. Press Done again when the signal is back."; leaving with unsaved changes asks first.
2. **F96:** the token is tried again on every return to the app, when the signal comes back (the outbox reaches the server), and every 30 minutes while the app is open, until this install's token is registered. After a day without one, Settings › Notifications shows "This phone isn't getting notifications yet. Restart the phone, then open BuildVibes."
3. **Activity:** the six kinds, each with its line ("Sara Al-Mutairi posted an announcement: Concrete pour tomorrow"), opening the post at its card.
4. **Remind me** (`flutter_local_notifications`):
   - in the note's menu, for a note, a to-do or a checklist: a day and time (the daybook's picker), saved as `remind_at`;
   - the phone shows "Reminder · Order 40 more sleeves" at that time, even when the app is closed; a tap opens My notebook;
   - the slip shows "Reminder · today 4:00 pm"; Remind me again changes or removes it;
   - done, deleted or removed: the reminder goes; on every start, the phone sets again the reminders still to come (a reinstall, a new phone);
   - a reminder is never sent to anyone else and never goes through the server.
5. **F98:** the person card at the top of a conversation's beginning: face, name, role · company, "Both in …" for the Spaces you share, and View profile. A request shows it with its strip under it. Messages stay anchored above the typing box.
6. **Strings** in English and Arabic.

## Questions for the lead, each with a recommendation
1. **OF's strip: when it shows.** *Recommendation:* only after a try to reach the server failed for want of a signal (the app has no other way to know), and until something gets through. Never a count of what waits.
2. **What waits with no signal.** *Recommendation:* send back, give to, an update, ticks and Got it / Received / On my way join what already waits. Page edits keep the editor open instead (their saves depend on the page's version, so a later send could clash), and meeting notes stay as they are.
3. **F97's follow-up wording.** *Recommendation:* "Omar Haddad · 2 new messages" with no text even when the person shows message text, since it stands for several; it replaces the first push on Android (same tag).
4. **Remind me's place and words.** *Recommendation:* in the note's menu, as `2557:35481` draws it, for every kind but a voice note; "Reminder · {words}" on the phone; the slip shows the reminder's time in grey (it's the person's own, not a due).
5. **F96's day.** *Recommendation:* the line shows when no token has been registered for 24 hours since this install first tried; Home's existing "notifications aren't reaching this phone" line (F9) stays as it is.
6. **Frames not drawn** (the Remind me picker, the phone's reminder, F96's line, F98's card, the editor's note). *Recommendation:* built from existing parts; the lead checks them on the slice 10 shots.
7. **The six Activity kinds' words.** *Recommendation:* as the pushes say them, with the post's title: "{name} posted an announcement: {title}", "{name} gave you a checklist: {title}", "{name} added you to a scheduled activity: {title}", "{name} shared a new version of {title}", "{name} made a change to {title}", "{name} cancelled {title}".
