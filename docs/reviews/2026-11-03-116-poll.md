# Review 116: Poll (proposal, 3 Nov)

**Status:** frames drawn, waiting for the founder's ok. The founder asked for it (16:52) as a "standard creative" poll: the behaviour everyone knows from WhatsApp and Telegram, in BuildVibes' site-paperwork look.

## The rule
- **A new post kind, Poll** («تصويت»), in the composer's kinds grid. It takes one question (2–140 characters) and **2 to 6 options** (1–60 characters each).
- **Answers:** "One answer · More than one" («إجابة واحدة · أكثر من إجابة»), one single-choice control (rule 6). One answer is the default.
- **Voting:**
  - one tap chooses; tap again to change or take back, until the poll closes;
  - with "More than one", each option toggles.
- **What everyone who sees the post sees:** each option shows a thin share bar and **the faces of who chose it**, never a percentage (rule 3). Your own choice is ticked.
- **"Not yet: Lina, Tariq and 2 more"** («لم يصوّت بعد: …») lists who could answer and hasn't: the members in the post's audience who may vote, Viewers included.
- **Closes:**
  - an optional "Closes" time, as a chip;
  - the creator, or a Space admin, can close it any time from the hold bar;
  - closing is final.
- **Decided:** when it closes, the option with the most choices gets a red **DECIDED · <option>** stamp («تقرّر · …»).
  - A tie stamps **TIED** («تعادل»), and the creator picks the winner with one tap, which stamps it.
  - Nobody voted: no stamp, and "Closed with no answers".
- **Make it a work item** («حوّله إلى بند عمل») on a decided poll opens the composer as a work item, with the question and the decided option as the title. The creator and Leads can do it.
- **Who:**
  - Lead and Crew create a poll;
  - Viewers vote but don't create;
  - audience as any post: Whole Space or a Team.
- **Notifications:**
  - a new poll notifies like an announcement;
  - the creator gets one push when it closes, with the result;
  - no push for each vote.
- **Edit:** the question and options can't change after the first vote. Before that, they can.
- **Not included:** anonymous voting, ranking, comments per option, and adding options after posting.

## Server (for the build)
- **Tables:** `polls` (post_id, multi, closes_at, closed_at, closed_by, decided_option); `poll_options` (id, post_id, label, sort); `poll_votes` (option_id, profile_id, created_at, primary key (option_id, profile_id)).
- **RLS:**
  - read for anyone who `can_see_space_post`;
  - votes only through `vote_poll(p_post, p_options uuid[])`: the caller can see the post, it is still open, single-answer means at most one option, and blocks don't matter (it's the same Space);
  - `close_poll`, `decide_poll` (for a tie): creator or Space admin.
- **Cron:** close polls whose `closes_at` has passed, every 5 minutes, stamp them and send the creator's push.
- **pgTAP:**
  - a non-member can't read or vote;
  - no vote after closing;
  - single-answer is enforced;
  - Viewers vote but can't create;
  - the tie flow.
