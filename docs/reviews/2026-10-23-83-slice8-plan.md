## What the server already has (migration 21, live 16 Oct)
- **`notes`**, owner only (not even a Space admin reads them): `kind` text | checklist | todo | voice; `body` ≤ 5000; `points` 1–50 for a checklist; `due_at` and `done_at` for a to-do; `pinned`; `remind_at`; `put_in_post_id`.
- **`note_files`** (photos, or a voice note's one recording; ≤ 10) in the private bucket `notebook-files` at `<me>/<note>/<file>`.
- **`save_note(id, kind, body, points, due_at, remind_at, pinned, files)`**: new or my own; the id is the app's, so a retry is an update; a deleted note returns null (the app drops its queued edit); 500 new a day (54000).
- **`delete_note`**, **`set_note_done`**, **`pin_note`**, **`mark_note_put(note, post)`** (a post I wrote).
- **`my_calendar(from_day, to_day, from, to)`**, up to 62 days: rows `work_item`, `checklist`, `activity`, `todo` with Space, Team, post or note, title, place, day, start and end, status, `needs_me`. No colour: the app gives it.
- The app already reads `my_calendar` for the To do strip (`dueUpTo`).

## App, in build order
1. **Notes on the phone.** `Note` and `NoteRepository` (read `notes` and `note_files` under RLS; `save_note`, `delete_note`, `set_note_done`, `pin_note`, `mark_note_put`; upload a recording to `notebook-files`). A fake for tests.
2. **My notebook** (NB1, on v13 paper). From Home's list-checks icon (`/notebook`).
   - **Header:** back, "My notebook", a calendar icon (opens My calendar, question 3), the **Only you** pill (a tap says "Only you can see your notebook. Nothing here goes to a Space until you put it there.").
   - **Masthead:** today ("Thursday 15 October").
   - **Pinned notes** on the brand-soft band, under the masthead.
   - **Slips:** today's notes first, oldest to newest as NB1; earlier days below, each under its day divider. Each slip's first line is its kind and time ("To-do · 8:02 am").
     - **Note:** the words.
     - **To-do:** a box (tap: done, struck through), the words, and its time in the action colour ("Today 4:00 pm"; overdue too).
     - **Checklist:** its title and points; tap a point to tick it.
     - **Voice:** play, the bars, the length.
   - **Writing:** "Note" opens a sheet: **one choice** of Note / Checklist / To-do (rule 6), the words, the points for a checklist, a day and time for a to-do. Long press or ⋯ on a slip: Put in a Space, Pin to the top (or Unpin), Edit, Delete (asks first, N7).
   - **Voice:** hold the mic to record, as in messages (2 minutes at most; slide away to cancel).
   - **No signal:** a note shows at once; it waits on the phone ("Waiting to send") and goes when the signal is back. `save_note` is idempotent by id.
   - **Empty** (N2's words): "Your private working area. Keep notes, checklists and to-dos for your work. Only you can see them."
3. **Put in a Space** (`2557:35481`). The Spaces I can post work in, each with its colour chip. Choosing one opens that Space's writing card with the note's words:
   - a note: a work item, its first line the title, the rest the words;
   - a to-do: a work item, with the to-do's day and time as its due;
   - a checklist: a checklist, with its title and points.
   - A voice note can't be put (it has no words).
   - Nothing is posted until Post. When the server takes the post, the app calls `mark_note_put`, and the slip says "Put in a Space". **The note stays private and stays in the notebook.**
4. **Save to my notebook** on holding a direct message (`2561:35808`) and a Space chat message: a new note with the message's words. "Saved to your notebook."
5. **My calendar** (`2557:35587`).
   - The week strip starts on the market pack's first day of the week (rule 5). Swipe it for the week before or after; the month above follows.
   - A dot under a day that has something. The chosen day is the orange pill; today is the default.
   - Rows from the chosen day to the end of that week, a divider per day.
   - A row's label: **Due for you** (a work item waiting on me), **Due today** / **Due** (other work items and checklists), **Scheduled** (an activity), **To-do** (my notebook; a done one is struck through). The paper clip when it needs me.
   - **A tap** opens the work item, checklist or activity at its card in its Space, or the notebook for a to-do.
   - **Empty week:** "Nothing planned this week. Work items, checklists and activities from your Spaces and your to-dos show here."
   - **No counts** anywhere (rule 3).
6. **Strings** in English and Arabic (glossary words; Western digits).

## Questions for the lead, each with a recommendation
1. **NB1 and its Arabic have the old hour spine.** *Recommendation:* built on v13 paper (no spine; the time on each slip's first line); the lead redraws NB1 and `2564:549` before the founder's slice 8 test.
2. **Remind me** is in the note's menu in `2557:35481`, but it needs a local-notifications package and a phone permission. *Recommendation:* leave it out of slice 8 and build it with slice 10 (notifications); the menu shows Put in a Space and Pin to the top until then.
3. **Where My calendar opens.** No prototype frame shows the way in (Home H1 has no calendar icon). *Recommendation:* a calendar icon in My notebook's header, beside Only you. Planning is one place: the notebook, and its calendar.
4. **Colours.** *Recommendation:* each Space keeps `BvProjectColors.forId(space)`, as its emblem; the notebook's own rows are slate, as the frame. Two Spaces can share a colour; the Space's name is always on the row.
5. **Photos in notes.** The server takes them; the prototype draws none. *Recommendation:* not in slice 8, to keep the notebook as quick as the frame; add them after the pilot if people ask.
6. **Writing a note.** The prototype draws only the post bar. *Recommendation:* the "Note" field opens a small sheet with one choice of Note / Checklist / To-do (rule 6), like the Space's writing card's kind chip, but without audience, owner or photos. The lead draws it if it should look otherwise.
