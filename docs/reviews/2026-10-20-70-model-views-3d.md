# Review 70: 3D model views in the pilot — 20 Oct 2026

The founder decided on 20 Oct: **3D goes into the pilot.** A published model view can carry a small 3D piece of the model (GLB). Site opens it in the app, turns it with a finger and taps an element to see what it is.

**Proven on the founder's PC** (NBK federated NWD, Navisworks 2027, plugin "Export 3D (test)"):
- A **10 m room-size box**: 647 elements, 351k triangles, 4.8 MB (2.2 MB gzipped), about 7 s to export. It renders correctly, and tapping shows the element.
- A **25 m box**: 1.64 M triangles, 22.7 MB.
- The whole basement (19 M triangles) is refused by the size guard.

Details are in the plugin's `CLAUDE.md`. Spec: `docs/product/model-views.md` → "3D" (below, also added there).

**Order:** after the current slice 2 work. Branches: `model-views-3d-db` (migration 29) and `model-views-3d-app`.

## Founder rules (don't change)
1. 3D is **optional per published view**. The publisher picks the scope:
   - the **section box** of the viewpoint; or
   - a **room** chosen from a list (if the model has Revit rooms).

   No full-model 3D.
2. 3D belongs to the same model view: **same title, same version** (V1, V2…). No new post, card or Activity.
3. Everything else in review 54 stands: Library only, a light issue, never auto-posted.

## Part A: server (migration 29, `model-views-3d-db`, not live)
- `model_views` gets:
  - `glb_path text`: `<space>/library/<file_id>/view.glb`, null when there is no 3D;
  - `glb_bytes bigint`;
  - `glb_triangles integer`;
  - `scope text check (scope in ('section_box','room'))`;
  - `scope_label text` (≤ 120, for example "L2 · 2.15 Plant Room");
  - `box_size_m real[]` (3 values).
- Bucket `space-files`:
  - `allowed_mime_types` + `'model/gltf-binary'`;
  - the 25 MB limit stays.
- `publish_model_view(...)`: add **optional** parameters with defaults null:
  - `p_glb_path text`, `p_glb_bytes bigint`, `p_glb_triangles integer`;
  - `p_scope text`, `p_scope_label text`, `p_box_size_m real[]`.

  Calls without them still work (the plugin already in use sends none). When `p_glb_path` is given:
  - the path is exactly `<space>/library/<p_id>/view.glb`;
  - it was uploaded by the caller;
  - the upload mime is `model/gltf-binary`;
  - `p_glb_bytes` ≤ 20 MB and `p_glb_triangles` ≤ 2,000,000;
  - `p_scope` is set.
- `is_referenced_file` and `may_read_file` know `glb_path`, as they know `thumb_path`: members who can read the view can download its 3D.
- `my_model_views` adds `has_3d boolean`.
- pgTAP tests:
  - the 3D file of a Team view is not readable outside the Team;
  - a wrong path, mime or size is refused;
  - a publish without 3D still works.

## Part B: app — the 3D viewer
- **Library card** of a model view with 3D: a small "3D" chip on the image.
- **Model view detail:** a "View in 3D" button with the size ("3D · 4.8 MB").
  - The download starts only on tap, so it never surprises a site phone on mobile data.
  - The file is cached on the phone after the first open (works offline).
- **Viewer** (full screen):
  - Technology: a bundled three.js page in a WebView. The page ships with the app as an asset; no CDN at run time.
  - The reference page is `..\BuildVibes Navisworks Plugin\tools\bv360-3d.html` (three r128, orbit, BIM view, tap-to-identify, KHR_mesh_quantization handled by converting positions to floats once).
  - Behaviour:
    - It opens on the **BIM camera** stored in the file's extras.
    - One finger orbits, a pinch zooms, two fingers pan.
    - The **"BIM view"** button goes back to the publisher's camera.
    - **Tap an element:** a bottom sheet shows its name, category and level, and the element glows in the brand orange. Tap empty space to clear.
  - Header: the title and version ("Plant room services · V2"), and the scope label.
  - EN/AR labels from ARB; RTL layout around the viewer. The 3D itself is not mirrored.
  - Low-end phones: pixel ratio capped at 2; if the frame rate stays under 20, it drops to ratio 1.
- No new settings.

## Part C: plugin (built by the lead, outside this repo)
The Publish screen gets "Include 3D" with the scope choice (section box of the viewpoint, or room). It shows the size guard before upload, uploads `view.glb`, and sends the new parameters. It also puts the real element names (Revit element, category and level) into the GLB instead of material names.
