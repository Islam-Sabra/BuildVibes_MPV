## What the server already has
- **Migration 20 (live 16 Oct):**
  - `create_from_message(space, message, parent, title, lines)`:
    - from a text message the caller can read (not reported, not by someone blocked);
    - one parent work item titled `title`, owned by the author, and one work item per line `{id, text, owner}` (1–30), under it;
    - all record the message; a Team thread's message keeps them in that Team;
    - Lead and Crew (`create_items`); all or nothing; the same parent id again returns it (a retry is safe);
    - the parent is a **target**: never signed, never checked, never proof; its progress is its work items' ("2 of 3 checked");
    - each owner gets one push for the batch.
  - `week_report(space, today, from, to)`, re-made in migration 23 (live): `needs_decision` (late, sent back, done and waiting for a check; never a target; with `needs_me`), `checked` (the Done photo and who checked), `targets` (`children.total` and `children.checked`), `coming_up` (activities and due dates in the next 7 days **from now**). Every member, each seeing only what they may read; at most 30 a section; names on rows, never per-person totals (rule 3).
  - `share_week_report(id, thread, week_start)`: a chat message of kind `report` with `messages.report_week`; whoever taps it opens the week report with their own rights. A retry with the same id posts once.
- **Migration 30 (live):** the daily report's notes; nothing new for slice 9.
- **The app has none of it yet.** The 0.9.0 app shows a `report` message as an empty bubble.

**No migration is needed.** The one oddity, "Coming up" counted from now rather than from the week shown, is handled in the app: Coming up shows only on this week (question 4).

## App, in build order
1. **Data.**
   - `createFromMessage` on the daybook repository; `WeekReport` (four sections) and `weekReport`, `shareWeekReport` on a report repository; fakes.
   - The chat reads `report_week`; a message of kind `report` is a new chat kind.
2. **Meeting notes (R3).**
   - **Where:** "Turn lines into work items" on holding a Space chat message with two lines or more, for someone who may post work (as S8m's "Turn into a work item", which stays for a one-line message).
   - **The screen:** the thread's header, the message as R3's card: the first line is the title (the parent work item), each other line a row with a tick box.
   - **Ticking a line** opens the owner picker at once (the walk's: you first, then the Space's people; a Team thread's only that Team). Its face shows at the line's end; a tap on it changes the owner. Unticking clears it.
   - **Post N work items** is on when at least one line is ticked and every ticked line has an owner. It sends one `create_from_message`, under ids chosen when the screen opened, so a retry is the same batch.
   - **Lines:** blank lines are skipped; a line or the title longer than a work item's title (140) is cut at a word; a line of one character can't be ticked. At most 30 lines are listed.
   - **After posting:** back to the chat, "3 work items posted" with **Open**, which opens the parent's card in the daybook.
   - **No signal:** the screen stays, the ticks and owners stay, and Post says "No signal. Try again." (question 2).
3. **The week report (MR1).**
   - **Where:** "Week report" in the Space menu ⋯, for every member, between Search and Daily report (MN1's order).
   - **The week:** the market pack's first day (rule 5) to six days on, read with the phone's days and midnights. ‹ goes back a week; › is off on this week.
   - **Sections** as MR1, each on its slip, each hidden when empty (an empty section never says "0"):
     - Needs a decision: title; "Omar Haddad · late by 2 days" (late since the due date), "Rakesh Nair · Photo missing" (the send-back reason), "Omar Haddad · signed as done Thu 2:10 pm"; the LATE / SENT BACK / DONE ink stamp; the paper clip on the slip when any row needs me.
     - Checked this week: the strip of proof cards with the Done photo and CHECKED, the day and who checked.
     - Targets: title, progress line, "2 of 4 checked · due Sun 18 Oct".
     - Coming up (this week only): the calendar leaf, title, time and place, or a work item's day, due and owner.
   - **A tap on a row** opens that card in the daybook.
   - **The foot line:** "Made from the daybook. Everyone sees only what they may read."
   - **An empty week:** "Nothing to report this week."
4. **Share in chat.**
   - The pill opens the Space's chat threads I can write in (Everyone first). Choosing one sends `share_week_report` with the week's first day.
   - **In the chat**, the message is a card: "Week report · 11 – 17 October" with **Open**, which opens the report for that week with the reader's own rights.
5. **Strings** in English and Arabic (glossary words; Western digits).
