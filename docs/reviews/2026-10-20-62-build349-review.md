## The two notes, decided
### N1. Live updates must not leak to non-members (close before the pilot)
Any signed-in user who knows a Space's id can see that something happened there, though never what. Rule 8 says work stays in the Space, and permissions are enforced on the server, so this is closed **before the pilot**, not after it.
- **Fix:** private Realtime channels with Realtime Authorization. A policy on `realtime.messages` lets a user join a Space's channel only when `is_member(space)`, and a Team topic only when they can see that Team. Alternatively, listen to `postgres_changes` only on tables whose RLS already hides other Spaces. The choice is technical and goes in the next migration.
- **Test:** a signed-in non-member who subscribes with a known Space id receives nothing. A member receives the signal within 5 seconds.

### N2. An admin acting for a departed author can unpin and cancel, but not edit
- **Editing** someone else's words changes what they said under their name, so it stays author-only.
- **Unpinning** is housekeeping: a stale pin from someone who left shouldn't block the top of the Space until it ends. So the server lets `acts_for_author` unpin. The app shows Unpin and Cancel to that admin, without Edit.
